# Ranked current lengths: source-passage audit

Audit updated: 2026-10-02. This is an editorial check of the eleven source-reported
numbers in the candidate rank. It is not an individual scientific approval of
the fingerprinted claim records or a common-method remeasurement. A rank
orders the published numbers admitted to this source set; it does not establish
that all sources used the same endpoints, axis, season, depth, or kind of
current system.

A [2024 observational Gulf Stream study](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41598-024-68461-0)
also describes a 2,500 km downstream path scale from Cape Hatteras, but its
reported observational analysis spans about 1,500 km between 70°W and 55°W.
This corroborates the order of magnitude and segment name; it does not turn
the textbook estimate into a measured, whole-segment reference streamline.

The two 3,000 km values share rank 6 because the candidate ranks the reported
numbers with ties. They do not have equal evidentiary strength. Neither this
audit nor the rank turns a source-reported approximate extent into a measured
streamline. The ten corresponding claims remain
`not_individually_reviewed` in the scientific decision ledger. The
[fingerprint-bound editorial index](../../research/ocean-motion-ranked-length-editorial-reviews.json)
links each passage finding to its exact candidate claim and appears alongside
the values in the screened atlas. The two 3,000 km measurement claims now
cite PICES and *Oceanography* directly. Their exact fingerprints changed with
the source, scope, locator, and source date. The superseded papers above
remain audit history, not measurement sources. These revised claims still
lack individual scientific approval.
