What this sequence establishes

These are modelled local coastal-to-offshore profiles. They include tides and weather-driven flow. A noon sample on the fifteenth is not a monthly mean, seasonal climatology or annual extreme.

Current width and whole-current length: unknown. No width boundary or current footprint is inferred from the displayed salinity and velocity. The coastal current and adjacent Atlantic waters can both contribute to this section.
