    How to read this sequence

    Each frame traces a frozen daily field from the same seed. The line is a diagnostic streamline, not an observed current axis or a moving water parcel. A threshold stop is a limit of the tracing method; it does not measure where the Gulf Stream ends. The orange line thickness is a display choice.

    Frames switch discretely. Unsampled days are not interpolated. Playback advances one observation at a time, with equal screen time despite uneven date gaps. Selected daily samples do not establish monthly means, seasonal climatology or annual extrema.
