Ordering by a number requires one metric, one unit, and a stated scalar field. Range-only references remain unranked. Shared units do not establish compatible places, times, or methods.

Ocean states are OSW’s editorial ocean regions. The evidence may describe an approximate route crossing or a point association; read its label before interpreting the relationship.

Follow modeled ocean motion

These views remain hosted by NASA. The regional filter uses display overlap between video crops and OSW regions. A crop does not establish that NASA identified a named current or eddy.

Keep the definition with the number

Reference-route distances, published reaches, width thresholds, depth levels, and thicknesses describe different things. Metres and pressure in dbar remain separate. Unknown is not zero, and a range is not automatically an uncertainty interval.

Eddy evidence references are separate from the named inventory. Their original identity and eligibility qualifications are preserved; this edition does not promote an unresolved registry match or establish a physical footprint. Compatible length, width, and depth are still needed before estimating volume.
