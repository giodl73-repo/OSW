What the audit found—and what comes next

Loading reviewed findings…

The first regional core calculation used the 50–100 m layer with filtering that never bridges missing observations. It resolved no monthly widths: there were no hours with filtered layer observations available jointly across all eight stations. This does not mean the current was absent. View the diagnostic or read the execution review.

  • Cross-instrument synchronization and common-layer overlap; the audited arrays retain their original timing.
  • Changing ADCP bin depths and pressure variation at nominal-depth point meters.
  • A common physical layer, current identity, velocity processing, and coverage of both current edges.

Nominal depth is an instrument label. Pressure variation is not converted to observed depth in the support audit. A successful audit does not by itself make a current dimension measurable.
