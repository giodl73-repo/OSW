Source interpretation notes
  • Most velocity-product variables lack unit attributes. The paper and archived author notebook supply the m/s velocity and metre depth labels used in the chart.
  • Original eddy contour latitude attributes incorrectly say “degrees east.” The original labels are retained.
  • The paper describes a different eddy filter and contour interval from the commented archived notebook code. The original outputs are preserved; the tracking pipeline has not been independently reproduced.
  • The source’s approximate velocity RMS uncertainty is not a per-cell confidence interval. Virtual eddy positions are not directly observed footprints.
  • Selected original members were checked by CRC32 and SHA256. The complete archive’s checksum has not been independently verified.
