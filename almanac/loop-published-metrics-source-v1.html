Loop Current: length metrics and separation dates

Two published summaries describe the length of sea-level contours connecting Yucatan Channel and Florida Straits. They use the same daily altimetry product over 1993–2021. They are not independent measurements.

SD describes temporal spread. It is not an error bar, confidence interval or annual minimum–maximum range. A mean of daily lengths also differs from the length of a mean path. These rows do not enter the whole-current length ranking.

Reported final separations

The paper lists twelve TOEddies separation dates during overlap with moored measurements. These are a subset of 48 separations reported across the full altimetry period. “Separation” means final detachment without later reattachment under that source's convention; it does not necessarily identify an initial detachment or an eddy's birth.
