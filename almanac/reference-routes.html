Measurement rules · v1.2

Define the branch, layer, season and start/end gates first. Measure the declared path with WGS84 geodesics. Separate source geography from OSW-selected vertices. Round regional estimates to 100 km; the envelope comes from specified alternatives, not a confidence interval. Shared calculation rules do not make different scopes identical.
