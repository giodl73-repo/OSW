# Oceans and Gas Giants

*Earth's oceans and giant-planet atmospheres share rotating-fluid mathematics.
They do not share geography, composition, forcing, or observability.*

## The legitimate bridge

Both systems are rapidly rotating, stratified fluids with turbulence, waves,
jets, vortices, and transport barriers. On a rotating sphere, the variation of
Coriolis effect with latitude can redirect turbulent energy into zonal jets.
The Rhines scale estimates where that transition becomes important.

```text
small turbulent motions
          │ inverse transfer of energy
          ▼
larger coherent motions
          │ rotation + latitude-dependent Coriolis
          ▼
elongated jets and bands
```

[Gallet and Ferrari 2021](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2020AV000362)
explicitly connects banded gas-giant jets with jet-like Southern Ocean flow in
a quantitative theory of meridional heat transport.

## Object correspondence

| Earth ocean | Gas giant | Shared idea | Critical difference |
|---|---|---|---|
| current/jet | zonal jet | narrow organized velocity maximum | ocean jets meet continents and seafloor |
| front | belt/zone boundary | property gradient and shear | cloud colour is not a direct temperature map |
| eddy/ring | vortex/spot | rotating coherent structure | composition, depth, and forcing differ |
| bathymetric steering | deep/interior constraints | vertical structure shapes surface flow | gas giants lack a shallow solid seabed |
| heat transport | atmospheric enthalpy transport | moving fluid redistributes energy | observables and thermodynamics differ |

## Where the analogy is strongest

- alternating jets and anisotropic rotating turbulence;
- jet–eddy interaction;
- multiple nested spatial scales;
- surface appearance that incompletely reveals depth;
- boundaries that organize tracer and heat exchange;
- ambiguity in choosing one object-detection scale.

The modern giant-planet synthesis distinguishes deep zonal jets from shallower
large vortices and emphasizes remaining uncertainty
([Showman et al. 2024](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-fluid-121021-040058)).

## Where it breaks

```text
EARTH OCEAN                         GAS GIANT

thin liquid shell                   deep compressible atmosphere/interior
continents interrupt flow           nearly unobstructed longitude circles
seafloor steers currents            no comparable shallow topographic floor
surface heating and cooling         internal heat plus solar forcing
salinity controls density           composition/phase/cloud chemistry matter
ships, floats, moorings, Argo        remote sensing + gravity/magnetic inference
```

The same mathematical vocabulary does not license one-to-one identification.
For example, a bright Jovian zone is not an oceanic warm water mass, and an
orange infrared feature is not automatically a heat-transport maximum.

## A productive comparison protocol

1. Match dimensionless controls: Rossby number, deformation radius, Rhines scale,
   stratification, and relevant forcing scales.
2. Compare object tests, not colours: velocity maxima to velocity maxima,
   gradients to gradients, coherence to coherence.
3. Separate observed level from inferred depth.
4. Declare whether the comparison concerns morphology, dynamics, or transport.
5. Record the first physical difference that could invalidate the analogy.

## A falsifiable comparison

**Hypothesis:** at scales where both systems are in a rotation-dominated,
jet-forming regime, diagnosed jet spacing should scale with a locally defined
Rhines scale more consistently than with an arbitrary visual stripe width.

**What would count against it:** after propagating velocity and length-scale
uncertainty, the predicted spacing fails systematically in one system, or the
apparent agreement disappears when the compared level, latitude, and jet
detection rule are held fixed. That result would reject this proposed transfer;
it would not imply that all rotating-fluid analogies are false.

## What OSW may contribute

The strongest common lesson is taxonomic rather than pictorial: both worlds
contain bands, jets, vortices, boundaries, and nested coherent structures whose
identity depends on measurement and scale. OSW can use a shared object-card
format to compare them without pretending they are made or forced the same way.

## Common mistakes

- Comparing colour palettes instead of retrieved physical variables.
- Calling every stripe a material band.
- Ignoring vertical depth and observational sensitivity.
- Assuming Rhines-scale similarity proves a common forcing mechanism.
- Treating a useful analogy as evidence of causal connection between planets.
