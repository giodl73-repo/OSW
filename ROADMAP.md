# OSW Roadmap

Status: active planning baseline, 2026-09-09

Applies to: `main` and the next public-site cycle

## North star

OSW should become an ocean-first atlas in which a visitor can move through one
continuous chain:

`place -> ocean object -> motion -> heat budget -> evidence receipt`

The site succeeds when an interested reader can begin with a recognizable map,
ask what the water is doing, see how that conclusion was measured, and reach
the source and limitations without reading the repository in file order.

Heat is the first worked property, not the definition of the ocean. Provinces,
water masses, currents, fronts, eddies, gates, events, vertical layers, and
budgets remain distinct object types with distinct identity tests.

## Operating rules

1. **Product first.** Each site slice must answer a consequential visitor
   question and produce a visible interaction or explanation.
2. **Evidence before promotion.** Conceptual, observed, derived, and
   model-screened layers remain visibly different.
3. **Motion before new zoning.** Existing organizational regions stay
   provisional until transport evidence can test their boundaries.
4. **No false closure.** Surface forcing, storage, horizontal advection,
   vertical exchange, mixing, diffusion, ice exchange, and assimilation are
   not interchangeable budget terms.
5. **One map, several scales.** Global orientation, province inspection,
   event anatomy, and control-volume budgets should connect without implying
   that one projection or partition is uniquely physical.
6. **Receipts travel with claims.** Every quantitative view must expose its
   source, support, time, depth, method, uncertainty or sensitivity, and known
   exclusions.
7. **Keep process out of the product.** VTRACE, role reviews, work packages,
   and readiness records govern delivery; they are not visitor-facing ocean
   concepts.

- M4 advances from open sections to control volumes and partial heat budgets.
- The marine-heatwave chain D1-D14 now connects duration, footprint, lineage,
  identity sensitivity, cross-product surface temperature, surface energy,
  fixed 0-50 m storage, and depth-integrated horizontal advection.
- In the current North Atlantic screen, 0-50 m horizontal advection is
  +171.21 W/m2, 72.5% of the +236.03 W/m2 storage scale. The crossed-system GFS
  surface term is +112.61 W/m2. Their partial residual is not budget closure or
  causal attribution.

Here, D1-D14 are the public marine-heatwave study-stage labels used in figure
and receipt names. They are distinct from the repository-wide `D*` source IDs;
the corresponding final source-register entries are D38-D51.

## Completed site slice: guided event anatomy

**Consequential question:** What do five increasingly physical screens reveal
about one tracked North Atlantic marine heatwave, and where does each screen
stop?

**Visible result:** add a first-class route from the atlas landing page through
five connected scenes:

1. **Event** — duration-qualified footprint and lineage.
2. **Surface** — observed/model surface-temperature bridge and its limits.
3. **Sky-ocean boundary** — net surface-energy screen.
4. **Column** — fixed-depth storage from 0 to 50 m.
5. **Motion** — depth-integrated horizontal advection and the unresolved
   partial residual.

Implementation is complete on `site/guided-event-anatomy`. The route reads its
headline values and boundaries from committed D-series JSON, retains a complete
no-script five-question path, and does not change Atlas 07 release status.

Each scene should keep the map in a stable frame, explain one question in plain
language, expose the relevant depth/time/support, and link directly to its JSON
receipt. A persistent evidence badge should state whether the scene is
observed, derived, or a cross-system/model screen.

### Acceptance gate

- The route is discoverable from the landing page in one action.
- Back/next, keyboard navigation, direct URLs, and reset all work.
- D12-D14 numbers are read from or checked against committed data rather than
  duplicated as untracked prose constants.
- Mobile layouts preserve the map, main finding, and limitation together.
- Every scene has a text alternative and does not depend on color alone.
- Scene, evidence-class, loading, and error changes are announced to assistive
  technology without moving keyboard focus unexpectedly.
- No scene describes the partial residual as vertical mixing, atmospheric
  causation, or closure.
- Normal OSW tests, JavaScript syntax checks, generated-SVG parsing, browser
  inspection, source registration, and `.roles` review pass.

Run the current automated baseline from the repository root:

```powershell
python -m pytest analysis -q
python -m unittest discover -s analysis -p "test_*.py"
python -m compileall -q analysis
node --check atlas/app.js
git diff --check
```

Pytest reports the repository's parameterized subtests; they are not a separate
JavaScript suite. Source refreshes remain explicit network operations rather
than part of the default offline gate.

## Science track after the guided slice

### Depth and time sensitivity

- Extend the fixed-column comparison to 0-100 m and 0-200 m where the source
  support is adequate.
- Compare endpoint, daily, and any defensible subdaily tendency treatments.
- State whether the apparent ranking of surface forcing and horizontal motion
  survives those choices.

### Missing-term audit

- Inventory accessible RTOFS/HYCOM native tracer-budget, vertical velocity,
  mixing, shortwave-penetration, and assimilation diagnostics.
- Prefer native conservative tendencies when available.
- If a required term is unavailable, publish the absence as a bounded archive
  result rather than inventing a proxy that is stronger than its support.

### Generalization

- Freeze an event-selection rule before choosing comparison cases.
- Add at least one non-event or weak-event control window.
- Test a second region or season before presenting the mechanism as general.
- Keep operational forecast, assimilative ocean model, reanalysis, and direct
  observation claims separate.

## Zoning track

The 56 province identities remain useful reference units even if the 11/22
organizational hierarchy changes. Future zoning should score candidate borders
against:

- persistence across season and year;
- normal versus along-boundary transport;
- pathway retention and leakage;
- heat-transport convergence coherence;
- surface-to-depth agreement or reversal; and
- sensitivity to gates, topography, projection, and detector scale.

The result may merge, split, move, or demote current regions. A visually tidy
partition is not by itself evidence of a fluid boundary.

## Proposed next research program: ocean-state exchange

The next research program turns every ocean-state border into a testable
hypothesis. It begins with source-edition control, a global province-adjacency
graph, and continuous depth fingerprints; then adds hydrographic contents,
normal and along-edge motion, three-dimensional volume and reference-relative
heat transport, seasonal boundary stability, and event routes. Its endpoint is
not a predetermined number of regions. Each tested border may be retained,
merged, split, moved, demoted to a display reference, or left unknown.

Permeability does not make the reference geography useless. Versioned
province × depth accounts can track inventories, cross-border flows, changes,
balances, relationships, event shocks, and revisions through time. This
economic-accounting analogy organizes stock and flow questions; it does not
turn provinces into sovereign, homogeneous, or physically sealed containers.

The first bounded slice is complete: a validated 54-province neighbor atlas
and representative continuous-hypsometry prototype now provide the geometry
and control foundation. Stage 3 adds a temperature-only ORAS5 model screen for
six Drake-sector provinces, with 96 depth-and-month property passports,
coverage and uncertainty kept separate, and descriptive adjacent-state
contrasts. It does not claim that adjacency is a barrier, temperature is heat
content, or a contrast is exchange. The frozen first boundary-exchange pilot
then selected `SANT--SSTC` from all 128 source edges before transport outcomes
were read. Sixteen native faces and one-for-one displaced controls now expose
inward, outward, gross, and net volume plus reference-relative thermal
transport by depth and month. The seasonal volume sign reverses and the
control behaves similarly, so the result is a method pass and warning against
wall-like borders—not a zoning endorsement. The independently frozen Stage 5
temperature-gradient test then found no static-edge match in any of 16
depth-month cases; detected surface peaks sit three or four native faces away,
and most deeper gradients fall below the declared floor. This demotes the
segment as a persistent temperature-front candidate while leaving its reference
identity and every untested field/year unknown. Stage 6 then projects the
existing North Atlantic surface heatwave lineage onto the state graph. Its
21-day dominant route remains in `GFST`; one small off-primary branch reaches
adjacent `NWCS`, but retains only 0.62% of its source pixels. The route therefore
adds state-address history without inventing transported heat. The active
program then completes its per-border evidence matrix without forcing a score:
127 borders remain physically `unknown`; one bounded `SANT--SSTC` segment is
demoted only as a persistent physical-boundary candidate; zero source edges are
changed. The bounded program is complete. The next scientific expansion is
multiyear, multi-property, and second-product evidence—not a prettier redraw.

The complete hypothesis, falsification conditions, stage dependencies,
deliverables, controls, visual grammar, and goal-sized first slice are defined
in [Ocean-State Exchange Research Program](plans/ocean-state-exchange-research-program.md).

9. `docs/vtrace/VERIFICATION.md`
10. `docs/vtrace/VALIDATION.md`
11. `docs/vtrace/TRACE.md`
12. `docs/vtrace/REVIEW.md`

Adopt one deliverable at a time and take each through OSW `.roles` review before
opening the next. The settled mixed specification baseline classifies 45
controlled items as current, target, deprecated, or unknown and maps every
accepted requirement. The settled Architecture allocates them to eleven
logical boundaries with a thin static reader, one-way evidence authority, and
federated domain admission. Fifteen settled interfaces now control the durable
records, addresses, compatibility, failures, and domain dispositions crossing
those boundaries. Settled Design and Code Rigor define how components satisfy
those contracts and how future changes prove them without creating a second
project-management product inside OSW. Settled Implementation Planning maps
all requirements and specifications into nine proposed packages. Settled
Verification allocates current and target proof without claiming unimplemented
fixtures pass. Settled Validation defines the required human and scientific
trials without claiming they ran. Settled Trace connects all 34 requirements
end to end while preserving pending and blocked states. Settled Final Review
authorizes only WP-OSW-001 pulse 001-A after the exact reviewed baseline is
committed and its entry packet passes. Later pulses, packages, validation, and
release remain blocked behind separate decisions.

### Pitfalls

The active `design/pitfalls/` register captures recurring structural failure
modes, not individual bugs. Its controlling domains are:

- **Scientific inference:** residual-as-process, transport-as-temperature,
  correlation-as-causation, and open-section-as-convergence errors.
- **Data and numerics:** grid staggering, unit/sign/reference ambiguity,
  interpolation, partial cells, time-window mismatch, and product crossing.
- **Cartography:** projection distortion, seam splitting, land overlap,
  schematic-boundary promotion, and color-dependent hierarchy.
- **Delivery:** stale generated artifacts, prose/data drift, broken deep links,
  oversized source payloads, and preview/public status drift.

A pitfall is only **solved** when a structural prevention exists and a test
would fail if that prevention were removed. Otherwise mark it open or
mitigated.

## Do not do next

- Do not add more top-level links without repairing the visitor journey.
- Do not choose or preserve 11, 22, or 56 because the number is visually
  satisfying.
- Do not convert the D14 residual into a named physical process.
- Do not use one new decorative projection as a substitute for interaction or
  evidence.
- Do not expose VTRACE stages, work packages, role machinery, or readiness
  vocabulary as ocean-atlas features.
- Do not add bespoke coordination software; reuse existing repository and
  portfolio tools.
- Do not promote the review branch by changing labels alone.
