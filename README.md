# OSW — Ocean States of the World

**Discover the world within the ocean.**

Explore its places, follow its currents, and see how it changes.
A fluid atlas of the world ocean.

[Open the project homepage](index.html) · [About OSW](about.html)

To explore the interactive pages locally, run `python -m http.server 8765`
from this directory, then open <http://localhost:8765/>. The site uses static
files; its maps read the datasets already in this checkout.

## Three ways in

| Explore the ocean | Follow the currents | See what's changing |
|---|---|---|
| [Maps, regions, and depths](atlas/) | [Currents, eddies, and connections](almanac/) | [Temperature, heat, and events](event/) |

Regions give us places to look. Currents and eddies show movement. Temperature
and heat reveal change. Explanations alongside the maps help you understand
what you see. You can start anywhere.

[Learn more in the field guides](guides/) · [Sources & research](research/)

## Available in this build

This checkout is a **working atlas preview**. It contains the Atlas 10 depth
ladder, motion almanac, [ocean depths](column/), and [exchange comparisons](exchange/).
Views identify their source, dates, and limitations. Dated datasets and model
examples are not a live feed, a forecast, or a complete heat budget.

These pages retain their working-preview status. Scientific-release decisions remain separate.
Some motion data remains subject to source-use review, as stated on its pages.
[About this build](about.html#status) explains the distinction between this
checkout and historical release records in [PREVIEW-STATUS.md](PREVIEW-STATUS.md).
