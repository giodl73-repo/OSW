About OSW

A world worth
looking into.

Ocean States of the World is a fluid atlas of the world ocean: its places, its movement, and its changes.

What are “ocean states”?

“Ocean states” is our atlas metaphor for the ocean's geography. Reference regions help us locate things; currents, water masses, and events can overlap those regions and change with depth and time.

These are not political territories or fixed physical compartments. OSW brings existing ocean science, maps, and explanations together; its reference regions do not establish a new scientific classification.

Find your way in

  • Explore the ocean for places, structures, and depths.
  • Follow the currents for currents, eddies, seasonal patterns, and connections.
  • See what's changing for temperature, heat, and dated events.

The same ocean connects all three. You can start anywhere, follow a question, and use the field guides whenever you want an explanation.

About this build

This checkout is a working atlas preview. It includes the Atlas 10 depth ladder, motion almanac, ocean-depth views, event explorer, and exchange comparisons. Their existing data and scientific scope have not been changed by the new navigation.

This build has not been deployed as part of the navigation update. Historical Atlas release and branch records are retained in the preview history; they should not be read as a live report of the hosted site. Those records describe earlier review branches and retain their original dates and release decisions.

Some research datasets still have source-use or review restrictions, stated beside their views. A clearer entrance does not change those restrictions or make a candidate a published dataset.
