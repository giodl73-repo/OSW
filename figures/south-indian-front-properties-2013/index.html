Source, method and limits

HYCOM GOFS 3.1 GLBv0.08 expt 53.X, 2013 daily 00Z source samples. August 14 and December 25 are absent. Native monthly means require common valid velocity, temperature and salinity on at least 80% of calendar days. Cross-depth comparisons additionally require every compared node to contain all available daily samples from the identical frozen cohort. Nearby meridians are model sensitivities, not independent observations. One year does not establish a seasonal climatology.
