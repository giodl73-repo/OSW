Scope and provenance
Native profile order and the different 120/240-bin depth grids are retained. A finite velocity is not an independent quality approval. Source coordinates describe ship sampling positions, not a current axis or width boundary. Source time stays in days from 1990-01-01 00:00:00; UTC and a calendar are not inferred.


The published transport reconstruction uses additional processing and model-assisted filling. Its 15–90 km by 30–200 m integration box is not an observed 75 km current width. Uneven ship occupations cannot supply annual minima/maxima or continuous seasonal playback.
