# EAF-Nansen original source custody

Kopte, R.; Tchipalanga, P. C. M.; Macuéria, M. F.; Ostrowski, M. (2016):
Physical oceanography during EAF-Nansen between10°S and12°S: CTD and ADCP data.
PANGAEA, https://doi.org/10.1594/PANGAEA.868683.

License: Creative Commons Attribution3.0 Unported (CC-BY-3.0),
https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/.

`eaf-nansen-original.zip` is the complete, unmodified provider archive.
`eaf-nansen-provider-index.tsv` is the complete, unmodified tab-delimited
provider index, including its citation/license and original download links.
`acquisition.json` records exact URLs, acquisition time, byte counts and SHA256.
Original files retain provider values, ordering, missing values and metadata.

OSW's separately labelled native JSON/gzip copies and source inspection view
are transformations, not provider products or a reproduction of the published
model-assisted transport reconstruction. They do not constitute current widths
or a canonical Angola current identity admission. See the frozen source-array
protocol and methods review linked from the inspection view.
