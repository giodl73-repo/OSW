# Tasman Front occupied-band description

1. Object: the zonal band containing the meandering Tasman Front and associated
   eddies, as synthesized by Nilsson et al. (1980), not one narrow jet or thermal
   transition. Retain this as a separate measurement metric.
2. Extract approximately 600 km from original PDF p27 / printed p23, section
   3.9. The nearby p26 / printed p22 discussion of at most 600 or 700 km is
   dynamical scale context, not an uncertainty interval or annual extrema.
3. Thermal transitions approximately 30 km at the surface and up to 120 km in
   the thermocline on p15 / printed p11 concern fronts around particular
   anticyclonic structures. Do not substitute them for this occupied band.
4. Do not transfer the older postulated 500 km zonal flow from p6 / printed p2
   into the report's measured jet width or manufacture a 500–700 km range.
5. No exact paired edges, cutoff, fixed measurement layer, dated width series,
   numerical uncertainty, map buffer or current/eddy footprint is extracted.
   Historical survey and thermal structure context cannot supply those fields.
6. Source mentions 33 or 34 S as band-center context. This does not authorize
   calculating a polygon or assigning a unique fixed centerline latitude.
7. The complete 73-page original is pinned locally, excluded from Git and
   reacquired explicitly using its checksum. Commonwealth copyright is printed
   on p2. NASA hosting is not treated as permission to redistribute.

Selected original pages 2, 15, 26 and 27 were visually inspected. No source
figure was copied or digitized; no numerical experiment was reproduced.
