# Linked factual-source use decision for ocean-motion v0.1.0

Decision date: 2026-10-02. Scope: 83 external source rows classified
`source_scoped_factual_claim` in the generated source review queue. Each row
supports only attributed names, numbers, or short paraphrased findings in
OSW entities, names, measurements, relations, or local observation records.
No article prose, tables, figures, source files, or publisher geometry is
copied under these 83 rows. This is a source-use decision for the present
package, not a license for the underlying publications or any future bulk
extraction.

The [U.S. Copyright Office's database guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/register/tx-databases.html)
distinguishes individual plain facts from original selection or arrangement
of a database. Its [writers' guidance](https://www.copyright.gov/engage/writers/)
also distinguishes facts and names from protected expression. The
[WIPO database overview](https://www.wipo.int/edocs/mdocs/mdocs/en/db_im/db_im_2.html)
notes separate protection concerns for substantial database extraction in
some jurisdictions. The OSW decision deliberately excludes article assets
and bulk source compilations. It does not settle provider contracts or
international database-right questions for a broader release.

`research/ocean-motion-source-use-reviews.json` records one current-use row
per source URL, with the policy reference and the exact use limit. Sixty-five of
these rows have DOI metadata, including a pinned Crossref response where
available. The other eighteen have reviewed source-page, agency, or institutional citations,
with an undated marker where the provider gives no publication date.

Seventeen other external source rows remain pending for rights: the 12 NOAA
MUNSTER daily products, the NOAA LSA velocity subset, the NAVO frontal
bulletin, the NAVO FREDDIES polygons, the Horizon Loop Current register, and
the credited cartographic service. Sixteen of those rows carry extracted
provider values. None is covered by this linked-facts decision. The separate
provider decision packets define the exact redistribution questions.
