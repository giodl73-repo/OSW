# WSC selected-core width: published extraction rules v1

Source: Dundas and Fer (2026), doi:10.5194/os-22-1003-2026, published final article, section 3.1, p.1008. This protocol records a source extraction after reading the reported value. It is not a prospective or blinded measurement experiment.

1. Keep the reported 28 km as a local zonal core-width statistic at the 77.25 N target transect, from the authors' selected high-transport sections. It is neither a whole-current width nor a width at a single fixed depth.
2. Retain 7 km separately as the author-reported standard deviation associated with western-boundary variability. Do not turn 21-35 km into observed extrema, a confidence interval, physical boundary uncertainty or a seasonal range.
3. Preserve the source operator: vertically integrated northward transport Q across the sampled water column; maximum sought east of x=275 km, x measured eastward from 2 W on the target section. The authors select sections whose maximum exceeds the 60th percentile before their representative core-width calculation. Do not replace this with a half-peak velocity operator, a 60 percent peak contour, a water-mass threshold or the fixed x=300 km transport-region boundary.
4. The exact selected section count, per-section widths, numerical threshold and geographic edge coordinates are unextracted. An exact implementation of the authors' estimator remains unreproduced; do not invent those values from the rounded width and standard deviation.
5. Published absolute geostrophic currents combine glider hydrographic shear and satellite surface reference currents. The paper reports suspect glider depth-average currents in sections 7-10 and uses satellite reference for all sections. Raw glider DAC alone cannot reproduce the width estimator.
6. The 2 km by 2 dbar display grid is not independent native resolution. Native mean profile spacing is 3.5 km with 2.3 km standard deviation, and objective mapping uses 40 km horizontal and 40 m vertical length scales. Sampling WSC and Front Current regions takes about nine days, not an instantaneous section.
7. Use exact Table 1 section periods separately from deployment/recovery dates. Gaps in spring and summer, incomplete sections and selection prevent annual width extrema and monthly climatological claims.
8. Do not average or pool this core metric with the 2015 property/transport stream-tube widths in the atlas. Do not transfer to the unresolved Spitsbergen Atlantic name. Do not buffer an editorial route or infer state intersection from this number.
9. Keep publisher PDF bytes, acquisition/license metadata and the dataset landing/API responses pinned separately. The paper is CC BY 4.0; cite Dundas and Fer for the published statistic and Fer et al. (2025), doi:10.21335/NMDC-1222822416, for glider data. No native glider fields have been acquired for this extraction.
10. The atlas may show the published point and explicitly labelled standard deviation plus observation windows and method. Seasonal playback, whole-current ranking and physical footprint admission remain disabled.
