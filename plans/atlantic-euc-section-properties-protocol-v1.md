# Atlantic EUC section properties convention v1

2026-10-08. Editorial extraction, independent scientific admission pending.

Voituriez (1983), Oceanographie tropicale 18(2), 163–183, Table II on
printed p168 supplies eight campaign/station core properties at 4°W.
Keep maximum eastward speed in cm/s and the depth of that maximum in metres.
They are not horizontal width, occupied geometry or transport-derived width.
Section 2 on pp164–165 describes drifting-ship Aanderaa profiling relative to
500 m assumed motionless. Table II's transport calculation uses eastward
velocities >=20 cm/s in the upper 200 m; those integration bounds are not
paired horizontal edges or a fixed measurement layer for the speed maximum.

Retain Table II and Table I date text separately. Campaign 7910 has June 1979
in Table II versus June 1980 in Table I. Campaign 7912 has November 1974 in
Table II versus October–November 1979 in Table I. Neither date is resolved.
Campaign numbering is not authority to silently repair a printed date.
The August 1978 and January 1979 Table II entries fall within broader Table I
campaign periods; do not replace the narrower property date with the period.
Exact days, station latitude, core uncertainty and occupation timestamps are
unknown. Figures use campaign categories in printed order, with no time-axis
interpolation, annual extrema, monthly climatology or seasonal playback.

Figure 2, printed p167, has August 1978 and April 1979 velocity contours.
Digitizing paired edges at an explicitly chosen depth/velocity definition is
future work. Do not use salinity contours as velocity boundaries. FAO Paper
292 (1993), chapter 3.3.2, contains an approximate 200 km background lead,
but its OCR web transcription and underlying width method remain unverified.
Do not attach the figure occupations or transport cutoff to that lead.
