# Somali pre-monsoon width rules v1

This supplements the general width protocol for source-defined seasonal regional
prose spans. It does not establish a standard measurement boundary.

Preserve the author's March–May calendar and 50–100 km approximate scale as a
single seasonal regional statement. Do not create separate monthly observations,
a midpoint, an annual minimum/maximum, confidence bounds or repeated occupations.
The northern shallow coastal flow and southward undercurrent remain distinct.
The southern EACC extension to 3–4 N does not define the northern width's edges.
No fixed depth, velocity cutoff or geographic edge coordinates are extracted.

Use `seasonal_regional_width_range`, `published_seasonal_regional_prose_width_span`
and `author_reported_seasonal_regional_current_scale`. Preserve original-source
and audit receipts. Numeric width remains null; range is [50,100] km; months
are [3,4,5]. Exact dates, layer bounds, section geometry and uncertainty are null.
Ranking, whole-current inference, annual extrema and width playback remain false.

Show the supported seasonal span and calendar separately. The nine other months
are unknown width evidence, not zero flow or absence. No monthly interpolation.
Existing editorial route playback remains independent; this width statement
cannot attach to those routes or imply seasonal map boundaries.
