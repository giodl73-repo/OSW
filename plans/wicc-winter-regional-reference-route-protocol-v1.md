# WICC winter regional reference route: source-stage protocol

Date: 2026-10-28. Status: frozen before route calculation; preparation only.

This estimates the length of an explicitly editorial offshore polyline between
10 N and 22 N, motivated by the two regional descriptions in the indexed
publisher abstract of Shetye et al. (1991), doi:10.1016/0198-0149(91)90087-V.
The study concerns December 1987 and January 1988. The abstract describes
poleward coastal flow, with its northern expression near the continental slope.
The original methods and figures remain unavailable. These latitudes are
regional bookkeeping gates, not established origin and termination points.

Use the existing reference-route generator and WGS84 geodesic segment sums.
All longitudes and intermediate vertices are OSW selections; no source curve,
isobath or measured velocity axis is digitized. Keep the latitude gates fixed.
Compare the declared nominal polyline with the same vertices shifted west by
0.15 and 0.30 degrees. These are offshore placement scenarios, not observed
positions, seasonal extrema, error bars or physical width estimates. Do not
tune a scenario after seeing a length. Preserve any failed land-check result
and revise the input under a new version if correction is necessary.

Require all scenarios to avoid the coarse OSW land mask when densified at
10 km or finer. This test does not prove shelf-break alignment or bathymetric
clearance. Keep state and NASA crop overlaps as editorial navigation joins.
Round displayed length to 100 km; retain exact computed values for reproducibility.
The scenario envelope describes only the declared longitude choices; it does
not bound unknown current-axis length or endpoint uncertainty.

Keep this candidate separate from canonical published lengths, physical
whole-current lengths and observed seasonal geometry. No width buffer, annual
range, transit time, parcel trajectory, seasonal animation or rank admission.
The southward undercurrent is excluded. Shelf and slope variability must not
be merged: Shetye et al. (2008), doi:10.1029/2008JC004874, section 5.2,
paragraph 26 distinguishes their possible flow reversals. Its roughly 700 km
remote-forcing distance is not a WICC length measurement.

Deliver source/access review, frozen input, generated candidate and map, plus
independent numerical and visual review. Canonical integration requires a
separate decision on abstract-only support and the regional route role; retain
the missing whole-current endpoint gate. No installed packet, index, WASM,
main branch or local bulk preview is changed by source preparation.
