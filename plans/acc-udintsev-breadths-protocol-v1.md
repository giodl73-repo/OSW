# ACC Udintsev breadth conventions

1. Retain the two original-source quantities separately: approximately 170 km
   between SAF and SACCF (enclosing the three major fronts), and approximately
   500 km between NB and SB. Neither value is an endpoint of a pooled range.
2. Location is the Udintsev entrance at 144 degrees W. Figure 5 reports
   meridional separation relative to PF; no flow-normal transect, exact paired
   geographic coordinates, digitized boundaries or physical footprint recovered.
3. Preserve CNES-CLS18 MDT contours: SAF -0.10 m, PF -0.58 m, SACCF -1.00 m;
   NB +0.30 m and SB -1.11 m. The latter boundaries are chosen from circumpolar
   streamlines passing through the northernmost/southernmost Drake latitudes.
   Jet speeds above 0.2-0.3 m/s are associations, not an adopted edge cutoff.
4. MDT reference period is 1993-2012. Assimilated observations spanning
   1993-2016, Argo validation during 2001-2017, cruises in February 2016 and
   December 2017, and particle tracking November 2015-April 2016 are distinct
   supports. No instantaneous occupation dates or monthly width series inferred.
5. No fixed depth slab, uncertainty interval, annual extrema, seasonal playback,
   current ranking, route buffer or physical OSW state intersection admitted.
   Spatial grid resolution, hydrographic front agreement and transport error
   cannot supply width uncertainty. Approximate qualifiers remain visible.
6. Drake SAF-SACCF separation around 270 km at 58 degrees W is a separate
   comparison, not Udintsev width support or a third range endpoint. No value
   transfers to Antarctic Slope/Coastal currents or individual constituent fronts.
7. Verify the original bytes, receipt and protocol. Bind each source row to its
   immutable identity in Python and Rust/WASM. Show metric names, contours,
   reference period and unresolved supports beside separate accessible graphics.

Source: Park et al. (2019), doi:10.1029/2019JC015024, original PDF pp3,5-8,
printed pp4513,4515-4518, especially p4517 and Figure 5. Selected pages reviewed;
no full numerical study reproduction or independent scientific review claimed.
