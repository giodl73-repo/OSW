# Jutland cited satellite widths: support convention v1

This editorial extraction uses the unchanged DMI Scientific Report 00-15 (2000),
a reprint of Mads Hvid Nielsen's April 1999 thesis. Selected original pages were
inspected; no full-report or original Aarup satellite-method review is claimed.

1. Keep the three section 2.4 estimates separate: Thyborøn 40 km, Hanstholm
   25 km, Hirtshals 20–25 km. The Danish passage attributes these to satellite
   images, citing Aarup (1994a). Points are not uncertainty-free exact values;
   Hirtshals is a reported regional span with no selected midpoint.
2. Aarup (1994a), *Satellitbilleder af danske havområder*, Havforskning 42, is
   distinct from the English thesis/review in series 52. The original imagery,
   dates, thresholds, sensor, pixel error and paired edges remain unreviewed.
3. Freshwater influence and a well-defined hydrographic current are alternative
   definitions discussed in section 2.4.1. Neither gives the actual image-width
   extraction threshold. Do not claim a velocity-core width or fixed salinity edge.
4. The mainly mixed / barotropic description and roughly 30 m local bathymetry
   in section 2.4.2 do not fix the layer for satellite-width estimates. Keep
   fixed layer bounds, occupation dates and numerical uncertainty null.
5. The 1994 wind/current record, 1980–1996 hydrographic sampling, German Bight
   fractions, nutrient transport and salinity filter are separate analyses.
   Section 7.3.3 qualitatively infers midsummer widening from water-mass fractions
   at three coast distances. No paired width edges or numeric seasonal widths
   are extracted; do not assign 40/25/20–25 km to seasons or animate them.
6. Different sites and sources do not establish a 10–40 km annual range. The
   DHI 2019 10–20 km coastal water-mass band retains its own geographic support.
   Its exact numerical phrase was not located in the inspected Nielsen width
   passage. The bibliographic link is established; numeric corroboration is not.
7. No full-current dimension, ranking, route buffer, inherited Norwegian Coastal
   width or North/South Jutland alias is admitted. Geographic site names are not
   exact section coordinates. Original source, acquisition, protocol, complete
   source row and naming context are pinned; source identity selects the audit
   independently of editable owner/context in Python and Rust/WASM.
