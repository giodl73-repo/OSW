reference-route rules to the accepted southern branch. This is cartographic
navigation and an OSW polyline measurement, not a recovered velocity axis.

southward flow along the eastern margin from approximately20S to Cape Sainte
Marie near25deg30minS. Paragraph6 leaves downstream continuations unresolved.

retroflection, ring and SICC continuations, and the equatorward undercurrent.

coordinates: (49.5,-20), (49,-21), (48.5,-22), (48.1,-23), (47.6,-24),
(46.8,-25), (45.3,-25.8), in longitude/latitude degrees. The southern point

independent latitude offsets -0.1,0,+0.1 degrees at the first and last point.

position errors or seasonal excursions. Keep the intermediate bends fixed.
