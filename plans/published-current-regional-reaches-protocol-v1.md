# Published current regional reaches: prospective protocol v1

## Quantity and claim

Retain an explicit primary-source statement of a current's regional spatial
reach as a dated, geographically scoped constraint. A reported threshold and
its comparison operator are inseparable. `gt300km` is not a point estimate of
300km, a finite range, a confidence interval or a whole-current length.

V1 handles the Arnone et al.1990 Algerian Current statement supported by the
abstract on printed1587 and results on printed1590 of DTIC ADA223991. Archive
PDF pages2 and5 were visually reviewed. The retained11-page archive copy is
missing printed1596–1597; conclusions have not been reviewed. Its full file
size and two provider checksums were verified. The missing pages are not
reconstructed or silently treated as available.

The source reports a near-coastal reach over300km between Oran and Algiers,
from combined satellite and ship work in1–22May1986. The ship-only cruise
ends21May in the methods. Use the declared combined study window without
assigning either endpoint to every observation. Publication1990 is not an
observation date. This is not an annual or seasonal envelope.

## Rules and rejection conditions

- Preserve source identity, locator, original hashes, page completeness,
  observation support, quantity, exact comparison and units.
- Require a positive finite km threshold, relation`gt`, absent upper bound and
  absent reported numerical uncertainty. Do not fabricate either missing value.
- The30km source context is proximity to the coast. Do not admit a width from
  this value, infer a0–30km current-width interval, or create a geographic band.
- Do not integrate a coastline or reference route, recover a velocity axis,
  join regional pieces, or infer whole-current geometry or length.
- Whole-current published length, physical length lower bound and rank remain
  unmeasured/null/ineligible. A source-reported regional reach is its own
  quantity; no physical centreline-distance bound is established by this rule.
- Reject changed claim support, units, date order, comparator, bound scope or
  source copies that do not match custody. Archive completeness must remain
  explicit. Source errors require a new scientific version after execution.

## Output and verification

Create one source-reach report and a schematic showing an open threshold with
an arrow toward unspecified larger reaches. The arrow has no geographic
meaning and supplies no finite upper endpoint. Include source/time support,
the distance meaning, incomplete-archive note, absent uncertainty and flags
preventing physical width or whole-current promotion.

Freeze producer, source facts, original, acquisition, protocol, synthetic tests
and preflight in Git before report/figure execution. The reported source
number was inspected as a fact; the producer performs no distance calculation,
unit conversion, statistical estimation or graphical digitization. Independent
actual-original admission checks follow the first frozen execution. Batch
canonical query/UI admission with subsequent compatible measurements; no
per-gap PR or full browser/regression cycle is required for source review.
