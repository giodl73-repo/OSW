## Evidence and rules

Auad, Roemmich and Gilson (2011), Progress in Oceanography 91, 576–592,
doi:10.1016/j.pocean.2011.09.004, publisher-indexed abstract reports a
500–800 km California Current width along the coast from 43 N to 23 N.
Store the span with a null scalar. Do not select 650 km as a midpoint or
interpret these bounds as annual extrema, confidence bounds or a coastal-jet
width. Argo and XBT periods are dataset context, not exact width occupations.

Ohshima, Wakatsuchi and Saitoh (2005), Journal of Oceanography 61, 845–855,
doi:10.1007/s10872-006-0004-3, publisher abstract reports an approximately
100 km Oyashio along the southern Kurils. Store the approximate scalar with
unknown numerical uncertainty. September 1999–August 2000 describes the
velocity-field study, not a width time series. Its 0.2–0.4 m/s flow speeds
are not an extracted boundary cutoff. Do not transfer widths from the
distinct Coastal Oyashio, neighboring Subarctic Current or warm-core ring.

Both sources were read at abstract level. Full original methods, figures,
edge definitions, occupied sections and exact width layers remain unreviewed.
No original PDF or acquisition checksum is claimed. The retained audit hashes
identify editorial extraction documents, not original publisher responses.
Exact observation dates, calendar months, section geometry and fixed layer
bounds remain null. Full-width inference, ranking, annual extrema and seasonal
playback remain false. Existing protocol bytes and canonical identities remain
unchanged. Full original inspection and compatible dated repeats are next gates.

are available through the existing Rust/WASM `widths` collection. Python pins
numerical values and scope exclusions; the Rust loader binds complete rows to
compiled audit documents and rejects coherently rewritten inventory receipts.
