# Current identity admission, version 1

An editorial identity admission establishes a separately named current in the
working catalogue. It does not admit dimensions, geographic boundaries,
continuity with another current, annual ranges or scientific approval.

1. Inspect a primary source's full text or original pages identifying the
   named flow and the proposed geographic, directional and vertical scope.
   Record the locator, access mode and source citation. An abstract alone can
   support the name when the scope is explicit; record that narrower access.
2. Compare the proposed identity with the base catalogue and pending proposals.
   Resolve duplicate IDs and specify distinctions from nearby branches,
   undercurrents, countercurrents, systems and eddies. Do not infer aliases or
   parent relationships solely from an abbreviation or shared source water.
3. Record the accepted label, stable ID, source-supported classification facets
   and unresolved facets. Source full-text availability does not imply a
   redistribution license. Link the source and paraphrase the needed facts.
4. Keep original-PDF/figure review gates for quantitative source extractions
   where required. They are not automatically name-admission gates when the
   primary text identifies the flow unambiguously. Name admission cannot lift
   a measurement, source-file integrity or physical geometry restriction.
5. Store admissions as a versioned extension to the pinned base release. Keep
   base rows and IDs exact. Export the effective named-current catalogue and
   merge admitted entity, name, source and length-assessment records into the
   shared native/WASM store. Existing measured data must remain unchanged.
6. Integrate each accepted identity into dashboard, atlas selection, object
   view, query and seasonal index. Missing geography stays unlocated; missing
   dimensions stay unmeasured, with no fabricated map or seasonal animation.
7. Pending-proposal counts exclude admitted identities. Historical proposal
   source records remain traceable. Existing incoming links must reach the
   accepted record and its measurement-scope notes.
8. Verify exact source queries, native/WASM parity, changed-record indication,
   keyboard access, narrow-screen layout, old-row preservation and rejection
   of coherently rehashed identity or measurement promotions.

The effective working catalogue is a draft data revision. This protocol does
not publish a release, change main, or authorize a scientific claim review on
behalf of an external oceanographer.
