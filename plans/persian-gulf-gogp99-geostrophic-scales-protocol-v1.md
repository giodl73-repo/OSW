# GOGP99: distinguish velocity scales, salinity cores and editorial routes

Use `persian-gulf-saline-overflow` for the reported Persian Gulf Water outflow.
Retain the existing six half-salinity-contrast core records without modification.
Pous, Carton and Lazure (2004), part 2, author-version printed p6 / PDF7,
describes geostrophic velocities near 0.1 m/s over 30 km and a narrow peak near
0.4 m/s, 5 km wide, on section R13. Record two approximate author-described
spatial scales, with distinct operators, not independent occupations or a
5–30 km physical variability range. Neither speed is an extracted edge cutoff.

Part 1, author-version printed p5 / PDF6 and Figure9 printed p22 / PDF23,
identifies the displayed velocity calculation as R13b. It uses a 30 m reference
level plus an added mean velocity of 0.09 m/s from earlier Johns et al. data.
The earlier March1997 reference and annual-mean offset do not date the 1999
widths. No ADCP data was available at R13/R12; D2 returned no data. These are
reference-dependent geostrophic estimates, not directly measured ADCP widths.
The authors warn about their accuracy and the realism of the surface inflow.
Keep widths' fixed layer, exact geographic edges and numerical error null.
The 45 m transport partition is not a width layer; the 30 m reference is not
a width depth. Figure9 was inspected, not digitized.

Part 1 Table1 printed p13 / PDF14 dates R13a, R13b and R13c separately:
1999-10-11, 1999-10-22 and 1999-11-02. Part 2's width description says R13
without a realization suffix. Preserve the R13b method/figure link and all
occupation dates as context; do not assert that either characteristic width
has a verified exact occupation. Neither seasonal nor annual extremes result.

The regional route is an OSW editorial sketch of the source-described northern
outflow/cascading reach. Two approximate geographic descriptions in part 2's
abstract (25deg20'N,57E and 24deg50'N,56deg50'E) constrain the sketch; all drawn
vertices and upstream truncation choices are declared in its input. The map
is not the R13 velocity section, a particle path, a salinity polygon or a
whole-current axis. Do not compare its length to source section-distance
coordinates or buoy travel distances. Scenario spread quantifies drawing and
gate choices, not physical seasonal variation or a confidence interval.

Pin both complete unchanged Archimer author versions and their receipts.
Their page/table numbering differs from publisher HTML. Copyright remains
AGU; PDFs are ignored and explicitly hydrated. Publish attributed numerical
descriptions and independent graphics. Python and Rust require the two new
definitions, preserve the six old salinity records, and reject reassigned
identity, invented velocity cutoff, dates, layers, errors, extrema or geometry.
