# Kuroshio Extension width and averaging convention v1

2026-10-08. Editorial extraction; independent scientific admission pending.

Sasaki, Minobe and Schneider (2013), JPO 43:442–456,
doi:10.1175/JPO-D-12-096.1, printed p445, describes an approximately
100 km meridional jet width in monthly mean fields extending to the date line.
Preserve one regional summary point. Its time operator is monthly mean, but
the paragraph supplies no sequence of dated width observations. Figure 1b
illustrates January 2005; that caption does not date every monthly-field
width in the prose. Observation dates and calendar-month membership stay null.

The same paragraph reports a 200 km meridional scale of the climatological
jet from Japan to 165°E. Preserve this separately as a contextual scale,
not a second width sample or a 100–200 km range. The geographic supports and
temporal operators differ. Farther downstream the broad climatological jet
reflects meridional shifts of the intense jet. Position displacement, a
regression anomaly's scale, and seasonal steric height changes are not jet
width variation. No numerical uncertainty or seasonal extrema are supplied.

Data context: monthly AVISO absolute dynamic height and surface geostrophic
velocity, January 1993–December 2010, 1/3° Mercator grid, Rio and Hernandez
(2004) mean dynamic topography. The study's 9-month low-pass analyses and
Figure 1a standard-deviation shading are distinct from Figure 1b monthly
mean SSH. Do not assign low-pass smoothing to a width series never extracted.

The methods section selects continuous 60–120 cm SSH contours, in 10 cm
steps, then finds maximum absolute velocity inside their search region to
locate the jet axis. Those contours are not stated paired width boundaries.
No transverse endpoints, velocity width cutoff, fixed layer bounds or
boundary coordinates have been extracted. Meridional breadth is not assumed
flow-normal breadth for a meandering jet. No width rank, route buffer,
occupied footprint, annual range or seasonal playback follows.

Show the 100 km approximate point with its time operator and a separate
200 km contextual-scale explanation. Pin the complete institutional original,
acquisition receipt and this convention. The institutional cover identifies
AMS copyright and restricts electronic republication; retain the original
locally ignored and ship only metadata and editorial extraction.
