# Southwest Monsoon Current: two July 2016 breadth descriptions

Source: Webber et al. (2018), JPO 48, 2259–2282,
[doi:10.1175/JPO-D-17-0215.1](https://doi.org/10.1175/JPO-D-17-0215.1).
Retain the complete CC BY 4.0 original and its acquisition checksum.

1. Keep the existing canonical Monsoon Current identity. Southwest Monsoon
   Current is the source's summer phase, not a new inventory name.
2. Transcribe approximately 300 km from original PDF p8 / printed p2266,
   section 3c. This is the northward near-surface flow in the southern Bay of
   Bengal July 2016 study, not the eastward Sri Lanka reference reach. Recirculating
   eddies contribute to the observed flow and transport.
3. Separately transcribe approximately 150 km from PDF p9 / printed p2267.
   This is conditional on using the subsurface salinity maximum to identify
   the component originating in the Arabian Sea. It is an origin-proxy
   interpretation; some of that component still recirculates. No clear separation
   between continuous and recirculating flow is established.
4. Do not pool the two numbers as a 150–300 km range or treat them as seasonal
   extrema, minimum/maximum widths, or a statistically measured width difference.
   The source descriptions have different layers and feature definitions.
5. The earlier 100–200 km values are transport-scaling assumptions cited from
   Wijesekera et al. (2016). They are comparison context, not observations or
   uncertainty bounds for either new record. The 3 degree and 300 km descriptions
   cited from other studies are not independent extractions in this batch.
6. Keep exact paired edges, boundary cutoff, fixed depth interval and numerical
   uncertainty unresolved. The ±4 Sv transport uncertainty is not width uncertainty.
   Campaign dates 1–20 July and Fig2's 5–15 July averaging are source context,
   not an extracted width time series. No monthly or annual width range is supplied.
7. Figure 4's daily fields are NEMO model velocity/salinity at 110 m; that
   visualization depth is not a common measurement layer for the two widths.
   No current buffer, occupied footprint, model-frame measurement, or width
   inheritance along the Sri Lanka route is permitted.
8. Bind both source rows independently of editable owner/context fields in
   Python and native/WASM. Show separate labeled graphics and source links.
