# Current source-prose extraction rules

These rules record lessons from the verified NGA2024 glossary review. They are
prospective extraction/admission requirements, not a claim that the literature
review was blinded or preregistered. Existing frozen measurement operators keep
their declared versions; any new numeric admission must state its rule basis.

1. **Verify the entry boundary.** Inspect the original page and column. A
   heading-based text scan can include the following dictionary entry. Algae
   length after Aleutian Current and binary-code timing after Chile Current are
   examples of rejected automatic associations. A candidate scan is not a
   verified current inventory or a numeric measurement table.
2. **Keep the metric direction.** A westward span at a current's widest section
   is not an along-current length. Source width wording alone does not prove a
   flow-normal measurement operator. Retain the original direction and locator.
3. **Keep spatial and temporal ranges separate.** Different widths at two
   meridians are spatial differences. Qualitative winter/summer descriptions
   are not numerical annual margins. Different regional seasonal statements
   must not be combined into one whole-current cycle.
4. **Keep qualifiers.** Approximate, possible and near-limit wording survives
   extraction. A statement that flow may widen to almost a value does not give
   a measured maximum, confidence interval or strict global upper bound.
5. **Verify the unit convention.** Preserve an unqualified mile as printed
   until an explicit applicable convention is established. A general statement
   that nautical miles are common in navigation does not settle every current
   entry. Withhold kilometre conversion and comparable ranking when unresolved.
6. **Bind the naming convention.** An explicit alias is evidence for that
   source/edition. Preserve its branch and geographic description. It does not
   automatically identify a modern physical current or authorize inheriting a
   different current's measurements. Coastal and Atlantic entries that the
   source separates retain separate dimension ownership.
7. **Separate publication from observation.** Edition year is not a sample
   date. Preserve unknown period, layer, boundary method and uncertainty as
   unknown. A navigational summary is not a dated field or seasonal footprint.
8. **Separate discovery from admission.** A previously unlisted source name is
   an inventory lead until duplicates, aliases, modern scope and required fields
   are assessed. New names do not automatically add distinct physical currents.
9. **Retain custody and review scope.** Record URL, edition, original checksum,
   PDF/printed locators and actual visual-review scope. Preserve private source
   originals where appropriate; publish our attributed facts/figures. Never
   claim a whole document or all candidate headings were reviewed from a few
   inspected entries.

The NGA review currently admits no identity, physical width, current length,
annual range or geographic footprint. Its raw candidate facts and source-name
diagram are evidence for subsequent assessment.
