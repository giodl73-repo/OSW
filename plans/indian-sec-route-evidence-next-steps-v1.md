# Indian South Equatorial Current route evidence

Status: source review; no route or dimension admitted. This work targets one of the eleven individual currents missing both a reported length and a reference route.

Carr et al. (2026), doi:10.3389/fmars.2026.1835248, supplies seasonal and interannual circulation context, public plotting scripts, and model deposits. Its schematic arrows and thermocline-ridge contours cannot establish a current axis. The reviewed source facts and discrepancies are retained in `research/carr-indian-sec-2026-source-facts-v1.json`.

The stronger next geometry lead is Chen et al. (2022), doi:10.1029/2022JC018969, section3.2 and Figure4. The publisher HTML explicitly defines a route by maximum surface geostrophic velocity and presents SON routes. This is a source-defined operator lead, not an OSW reproduced axis. Original figure pixels, numerical axes, extraction details and endpoints remain unverified. The ordinary publisher ePDF request returned403 during this review; no bypass was attempted. A separate PDF, supplement or usable numerical route has not been acquired.

## Admission sequence

1. Acquire and visually review the original Chen Figure4 and its methods through a public publisher or author deposit. Retain hashes, labels, calibration and season/year identities. Seek numerical route coordinates or code first.
2. Establish exactly which velocity maximum is used, its search band, direction and continuity rule. A generic velocity maximum can jump to an eddy or a different branch. Do not silently substitute a salinity front, SSH contour or regional map arrow.
3. Freeze a regional gate convention with a documented rationale. Shared visible coverage may support an explicitly editorial regional comparison, but not physical whole-current endpoints. Never assign crop limits as physical endpoints.
4. If source-graphic tracing is necessary, freeze projection calibration, trace selection, gaps/crossings, resolution sensitivity and reading allowance before tracing. Preserve independent years separately from climatology. All SON cases remain one season; they do not establish annual minima or maxima.
5. Apply the existing WGS84 ordered-polyline protocol. Show scope, method, gates, date/season and uncertainty beside every number. Keep reference-route estimates separate from reported whole-current lengths in ranking and queries.
6. Deliver an atlas route overlay and current card with the same provenance and explicit regional status. Enable animation only for comparable dated geometries; do not interpolate unmeasured months.
