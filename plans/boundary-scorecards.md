# Boundary scorecards after the completed exchange pilot

Status: research intake; no score assigned from current evidence

Date: 2026-09-12

## Starting state

The completed exchange program leaves 127 source edges physically unknown and
demotes only the tested `SANT--SSTC` segment as a persistent temperature-front
candidate. This is not a ranking dataset and cannot be expanded by filling
unknown cells with inferred scores.

## Scorecard requirements

Each candidate edge requires separately stored evidence for:

1. seasonal and interannual positional persistence;
2. normal versus along-edge volume and reference-relative heat transport;
3. pathway retention/leakage against displaced controls;
4. matched hydrographic contrasts for temperature, salinity, density, and
   oxygen where support exists;
5. surface-to-depth agreement/reversal;
6. sensitivity to gate placement, topography, grid, product, and detector
   scale.

## Decision grammar

No scalar composite is produced until all admitted dimensions have a declared
normalization and negative control. Per edge, each dimension is `supported`,
`contradicted`, `mixed`, or `unknown`; disposition remains retain, merge,
split, move, demote-to-display-reference, or unknown. A missing diagnostic is
not neutral evidence.
