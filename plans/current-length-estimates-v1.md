# Length estimates for every current

The delivery target is one documented length estimate for every one of the 111
catalogued current identities. Additional speed, depth, width and migration
collections are outside this batch. A source-backed approximation is useful;
scientific measurement admission is a separate decision.

The length register brings existing reported lengths and calculated editorial
routes into one view. It preserves their definitions and source records. Its
initial empty values are a worklist, not an acceptable final deliverable.

1. Use a reported current/branch extent when one is already retained, and show
   exactly which current interpretation it covers.
2. Otherwise estimate a declared reference route with WGS84 geodesic leg sums.
   Retain coordinates, endpoint choices, source context and rounding. A nominal
   route estimate can be useful without pretending to be a measured fluid axis.
3. Define and complete the extent for currents that currently have only a
   regional study reach. Keep the regional calculation as supporting evidence;
   do not relabel its endpoints as the whole current's endpoints.
4. Show alternative phase/branch routes. When selecting from existing reference
   extents, use the longest declared reference extent as a reproducible display
   convention, with all alternatives visible. It is not a measured annual maximum.
5. For a family or network, explicitly declare whether the estimate measures a
   principal route or a sum of distinct member routes. Show components, prevent
   double counting, and label that convention alongside the number. Never
   silently borrow a member's length or imply a continuous cross-basin current.
6. Preserve historical-name or hypothesized-route interpretations. Estimates
   under those interpretations must say so. They do not establish an alias or
   confirm a circulation hypothesis.
7. Round geographic estimates to meaningful precision. Endpoint/route alternatives
   produce a sensitivity interval; source uncertainty and seasonal extrema stay
   separate and remain unknown when unsupported. Do not invent a universal
   curvature factor or percentage error bar.
   A declared geographic passage control may stay fixed during drawing probes
   when moving it would shift the handoff onto land. Identify the fixed vertex
   and its rationale. Retain failed earlier grids and every case in the revised
   grid; never select only the convenient numerical outcomes.
8. Deliver the completed register as JSON, CSV, and a sortable length-first view.
   Every number must link to its method, extent and source/route record. Preserve
   the canonical scientific measurements and their published ranking unchanged.

For the eight catalogued cross-basin families, the display convention is the
longest nominal extent among an explicit list of admitted basin members. Retain
all member alternatives and identify the selected member beside the method.
This is a principal-member length, not a global continuous axis or the sum of
member lengths. Unresolved aliases do not enter the member inventory. For the
Gulf Stream System, sum only the strict Florida Current, Gulf Stream proper and
North Atlantic reference segments, preserving their disjoint handoff conventions
and mixed source methods. Neither convention creates a canonical measurement.

Completion requires all 111 identities to have a positive estimate under an
explicit convention, every calculation and source to be reproducible, and the
ranked view and current links to be checked. A regional-only number or a hidden
missing value does not satisfy completion.
