# Source-reported eddy positions

Retain a dated source position when the source explicitly ties the name to
the coordinate pair. Keep this dataset separate from size/duration coverage
and physical geometry. A source name and nearby date do not prove a registry
physical identity crosswalk. Deviant / LC ring D is an explicit source alias;
the historical registry separation remains context only.

Preserve each native coordinate, hemisphere and reported decimal precision.
For a navigation candidate, convert west/south to negative longitude/latitude
and label coordinate order. Do not invent a geodetic datum, spatial accuracy,
uncertainty ellipse or decimal precision. A rounded integer latitude is not
an exact center and does not establish a plus/minus half-degree error bound.

Keep the source image date distinct from publication, survey and separation
dates. The 24 March 1997 Deviant center comes from the report prose describing
an SST image. Figure 5.4-1 is a survey of the northern part of another warm
circulation; it does not supply a whole Deviant section or boundary.

Do not transfer a neighboring feature's dimensions or coordinates. In this
case 400 km belongs to Fourchon; 50-100 km describes general survey circulation
scales; the second center at 28 N, 87 W is unnamed. No size gap is closed.

Historical navigation candidates are neither current locations nor occupied
polygons. State containment, tracks, interpolation, seasonal playback, ranking
and release-rights eligibility remain disabled. Raw original source assets
stay outside the publication. UI/query integration must be separately verified
before claiming these positions are available in the interactive atlas.
