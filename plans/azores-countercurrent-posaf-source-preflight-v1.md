2026-10-18. Existing indexed primary passages, abstract and the earlier
coordinate-meaning audit are known. No complete original PDF or Figure 9
pixels have been inspected in this follow-up. This is exploratory work,
not a blinded preregistration.

1. Recover Alves, Gaillard, Sparrow, Knoll and Giraud (2002), Circulation
   patterns and transport of the Azores Front-Current system, DSR II49,
   3983-4002, doi:10.1016/S0967-0645(02)00138-8. Discovery metadata identifies
   GEOMAR item1875. Verify the actual title/authors and publication labels;
   do not accept another paper or assume the repository's edition label.
2. Use ordinary public institutional HTTPS with certificate verification,
   a bounded response and PDF magic check. Preserve original bytes, exact
   final/request URLs, UTC acquisition time, SHA256, byte/page counts and
   edition/access findings. Do not replace any prior original or receipt.
   Original PDF, page images and discovery text stay ignored.
3. Review the methods, mooring geometry, Table3 and Figure9 before deciding
   whether the source actually resolves paired boundaries. The older
   100-250km interval remains section coordinates unless the original
   explicitly supplies a boundary interpretation. Do not overwrite that
   historical audit's access claim or turn its subtraction into a width.
4. The initial fixed figure-reading test is the POSAF temporal-mean
   westward1cm/s contour at800dbar in Figure9, motivated by the already-read
   abstract's approximately2cm/s maximum near800dbar. Record the panel's
   sign convention, averaging support and axis units before reading either
   crossing. Require two explicitly identifiable crossings of that same
   contour/pressure on the same connected westward core. No threshold/depth
   change, curve extrapolation, clipped-side doubling or axis-edge closure.
5. If both sides are readable, calibrate source horizontal distance against
   at least two axis ticks, retain raster coordinates and bounds covering
   stroke thickness plus one native raster pixel. The difference is a local
   interpolated-section component span. Round to10km; record plot-reading
   allowance separately from unknown sampling/model/velocity uncertainty.
   If units, sign, contour, pressure or either side is ambiguous, preserve
   the reason and a null span. A PDF render does not increase source data
   resolution or resolve boundaries between sparse instruments.
6. Keep the source's short1999 temporal mean distinct from a dated snapshot,
   monthly averages, the2009section, climatology or annual extrema. Do not
   map boundaries without verified source georeferencing. No full-current
   width/length, flow-normal interpretation, ranking, seasonal playback or
   physical state intersection is admitted by original-source recovery.
