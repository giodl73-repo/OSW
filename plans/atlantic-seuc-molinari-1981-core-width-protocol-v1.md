# Atlantic SEUC core width: Molinari et al. (1981)

The paper explicitly distinguishes the Atlantic South Equatorial Undercurrent
from the equatorial undercurrent and the mainly surface countercurrents.
Use only `atlantic-south-equatorial-undercurrent`; no cross-basin or family
inheritance. The existing Fischer2008 float study remains a different source.

Record approximately 120 km from printed p453/PDF p3: the authors define the
core by the 20 cm/s isotach and report this core width in eastern and western
Atlantic sections. This is an author-reported core scale, not whole-current
width, net-flow boundary, exact flow-normal span or width at every depth.
The authors say their oxygen sampling cannot delineate net-flow boundaries.

The abstract's approximately 100 km description is preserved as separate,
unreconciled source context. Do not average to 110 km, create a 100–120 km
range, assign uncertainty from the discrepancy, or count two independent
occupations. The body statement has the explicit core-contour definition.

Six cruises in 1978–1980 cover western sections at 25–29 W and eastern
sections at 4 W. Section dates in figures are not one date for the summary
width. No per-cruise widths, seasonal endpoints or annual extrema are assigned.
Keep observed_period and fixed_layer_bounds_m null. Core depths of 150–200 m
and vertical extent from about 100 m to maxima of 200 to >300 m are context.
