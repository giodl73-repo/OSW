# Eddy size and duration register

Build a lightweight research register for all 136 source-scoped named records.
These are labels and source identities, not 136 confirmed distinct physical
eddies or a complete global census. Preserve the existing full-candidate scope;
the 96 Horizon-derived labels remain excluded from the separate rights-screened
release. This work does not change that release or install a new runtime.

## Measurement rules

1. Keep individual events, recurring regions and families distinct. Candidate
   name crosswalks are links for investigation, never identity merges or copied
   measurements. Every normalized quantity retains its exact source pointer,
   input hash, definition, unit, dates, scope and raw source record.
2. Distinguish outer-contour radius, coherent-core radius, maximum-speed radius,
   integration scale, reported diameter and population statistics. Preserve
   source terminology and operator ambiguity. Do not derive diameter, occupied
   disk area, volume or state intersection from a scalar radius.
3. Conflicting source values stay unresolved with their separate claims. Do not
   average them, prefer one silently or turn disagreement into uncertainty.
   Keep standard deviations separate from measurement uncertainty and extrema.
4. Retain source-reported lifetime, source track span, coherent-core analysis
   interval, observation window, separation date and recurrent-region event
   statistics as different quantities. A figure window supplies no lifetime.
   Elapsed days are end minus start, not an inclusive observation count or proof
   of birth/death or continuous observations. Retain split/merger qualifications.
5. Preserve approximate years and months in their source units. No 30-day month,
   calendar-year conversion, inferred start date or age from a lone event date.
   Annual occurrence days can comprise multiple events; they are not one event's
   lifespan. A strict travel lower bound stays strict.
6. Population size statistics belong to the family and sampled longitude/cohort.
   They are not measurements of its named members or a basinwide seasonal cycle.
   Record changing sample counts and method/caption discrepancies explicitly.
7. Search and filter all identities, with source details and CSV/JSON export.
   Default order is by name. This first register offers no numeric ranking;
   future ordering requires a reviewed definition and unit, respecting source
   exclusions. Individual, region and family records remain visibly classified.
8. No interpolation or seasonal animation from sparse sections or dates. Future
   individual animations require dated tracks; regional seasonal summaries need
   compatible repeated observations. Satellite detection-to-name joins require
   explicit product/version, time, position and identity review.

Reuse retained small source JSON first. Add the visually verified Guerra et al.
(2018) Table 1 population radius values, preserving its caption's definition and
the methods/caption ambiguity. Keep existing Astrid, Agulhas-radius and Black Sea
recurrence protocols authoritative for their records. Work sequentially on one
low-priority CPU. Browser automation, runtime parsing and ocean-field processing
remain cloud-only. Publish on the existing working branch, preserving main and
all installed runtime leaves. This first register leaves unfilled gaps explicit.

## Continuing source batches

An explicit batch index pins each additional source-facts file. Every batch
binds the complete preceding evidence list before appending records, preserving
the earlier 48 records as well as the original 40. Source ranges stay range-only;
native percentages, weeks, depth bands and angular displacement retain their
units. A depth presence band supplies no lens thickness. Source-reported bounds
remain typed descriptions, with physical-limit caveats. Model radii retain the
published multipliers, deformation radii, product and rounding rule; they are not

describe elapsed dates between named reported stages, not an eddy lifespan or
continuous detachment. Qualified indexed passages retain their limited source
access and unreviewed-method flags. Numeric reference coverage does not mean

## Plot-reading protocol v1

For a legible original published plot with defined metric and axes, keep graph
estimates separate from printed quantities. Bind the original PDF and native,
unmodified embedded raster by SHA-256. Retain source page/figure, color and case
label, inclusive pixel selection, axis anchors and per-column trace extrema.
Exclude legends, labels and other curves. Visually inspect the source raster and
the numeric result. A clipped trace or ambiguous ownership leaves a gap.

Use a linear axis calibration for these seven DWDE radius cases. Compute the
selected trace's vertical envelope, add a 5 km reading allowance at both ends,
and round the lower end down and upper end up to 10 km, clamping at zero. This
allowance covers graph reading only; it is not an instrument error or confidence
interval. It deliberately avoids precision beyond the source figure. Preserve
unrounded values for arithmetic audit, not as headline measurements. The mean
radius is centroid-to-contour distance averaged around the maximum-circulation
contour; do not convert it to diameter or area.

Keep A1, A2, A1A2, B1 and B2 as separate child/merger cases; do not pool their
extrema or assign them to an intact parent. For Revelle select only the first
approximately 1-40 plotted days, before its documented day-51 split. Quantum II
uses only its displayed curve, whose endpoint precedes the report's later RAFOS
sampling endpoint. Display approximate support on the plot's days-since-detachment
axis; infer no calendar endpoints or observations between samples. These are
selected-segment envelopes, not full lifetime, seasonal or annual extrema and
not animation-ready series. Preserve the preceding 263 records exactly.
