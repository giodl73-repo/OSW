# Zeehan width source separation v1

Keep the approximately 40 km historical western Bass Strait slope width
reported in Cresswell (2000), Introduction p21, separate from that paper's
March/December 1997 Strahan sections. The historical description cites Baines,
Edwards & Fandry (1983), DOI 10.1071/MF9830155. It is one attributed regional
summary, not two independent widths or a whole-current representative width.

The complete Cresswell institutional PDF is checksum pinned and locally ignored.
Title/p21, instrumentation/Figure 1 p22, section description p25 and Figure 5C
p28 were visually reviewed. Baines archive text was readable through web PDF
extraction; publisher download returned 403 and archive download 404. No complete
Baines original bytes or independently digitized boundaries are claimed.

For Strahan, preserve March and December 1997 month precision, station labels,
qualitative width/strength comparison and source locators. The whole-voyage
windows (19–27 March / 1–9 December 1997) are context; exact Strahan occupation
days remain unknown. Neither section is assigned the historical 40 km value.
Figure 1's visually read year is 1997; OCR “1991” is an extraction error.

No numerical section widths, width ratio, interpolated monthly dimensions,
annual extrema, confidence bounds or occupied current geometry are admitted.
The 300 m ADCP coverage, lowest 15% exclusion, mooring isobaths and 15 m drifter
drogue depth do not define the historical width's vertical layer. Deeper-than-300
m flow is a coverage limitation, not a paired width boundary. Positive/negative
ADCP components and dashed zero contours describe plotted velocity signs; they
do not by themselves establish a reproducible whole-current width operator.

Keep the 2007 qualitative seasonal calendar and the 2004 static editorial
reference route independently sourced. A pair of 1997 occupations is not an
annual climatology. Any future dimension ranking requires the main width
protocol's compatible layer, axis, boundary and uncertainty evidence.
