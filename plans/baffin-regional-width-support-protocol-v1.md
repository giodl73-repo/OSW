# Baffin regional width supports, v1

Fissel, Lemon and Birch (1982), Arctic 35(1), 180-200,
doi:10.14430/arctic2318. Retain the unchanged institutional PDF locally,
checksum-pinned and excluded from Git; no redistribution license established.

## Measurement rules

1. Printed p198 / PDF19 reports about 25 km east of Devon Island. Preserve
   one approximate regional point, with no invented uncertainty or interval.
   The separate 20-25 km offshore center position is not a width range.
2. The same summary reports an average width of 10-30 km during the Lancaster
   Sound intrusion. Preserve the author's regional span without a midpoint.
   A possible factor-of-two widening along the excursion describes spatial
   change; do not multiply endpoints or assign a seasonal range.
3. Summer 1978/1979 are study context, not exact occupations for these summary
   widths. Winter current-meter records do not supply winter widths. No month
   labels, annual extrema or seasonal playback are admitted.
4. Printed pp182-183 / PDF3-4 describe drifters, CTDs and current meters.
   Near-surface 4-11 m instrument depths are not a fixed current-width slab.
   Dynamic topography referenced to 300 dbar is not absolute surface velocity
   and the reference is explicitly not a level of no motion. Neither is an
   edge definition. Speeds or speed agreement errors are not width cutoffs
   or numerical width uncertainty.
5. Printed p189 / PDF10: A-A' drifter crossings 6-16 km offshore, B-B' sampled
   section 35 km and C-C' passages spanning 33 km are sample envelopes.
   Do not identify these with simultaneous full current width or interval
   endpoints. Coastal confinement within 100 km is a separate location claim.
6. Printed p199 / PDF20 describes approximately 20 km diameter Bylot eddies
   and flow 30-50 km offshore. Neither quantity is current width; unnamed
   eddy occurrences do not create named inventory identities or footprints.
7. Both summers had unusually severe sea ice; typical-year applicability is
   unresolved. Do not extrapolate the source to all seasons or the full route.
8. The existing editorial Baffin route omits the Lancaster intrusion. Do not
   attach its width span as a geographic band to that route. Source diagrams
   are width scales only; no paired boundary coordinates were extracted.

## Admission and verification

Two source-specific records remain editorial regional summaries, ineligible
for whole-current ranking, full-width inference, annual extrema and playback.
Selected text pages and figures were inspected; a full-paper review and raw
field reconstruction are not claimed. Pin the original, acquisition receipt,
protocol, audit and full records. Array pointers bind each record separately.
Python and compiled Rust/WASM must reject scope promotion, context deletion
and identity transfer, even with internally recomputed packet receipts.
Independent scientific review and compatible repeated edge measurements are
required before physical whole-current or seasonal dimensions are admitted.
