# West Spitsbergen stream-tube width extraction v1

2026-10-08. Supplement to the existing OSW width measurement protocol.
Editorial transcription of Kolås and Fer (2018), Table 2, printed p.1611;
methods §§3.2–3.3, pp.1606–1608. Not canonical scientific admission.

Store six source table cells, keeping section A/B/C and tube 1/2 distinct.
These are local along-section spans of an Atlantic Water stream tube, not a
fixed-depth current envelope or necessarily perpendicular-to-flow width.
Assign the source's WSC evidence to `west-spitsbergen`; do not automatically
transfer it to the distinct ledger name `spitsbergen-atlantic`.

Tube 1 edges are the first points on either side of the maximum transport
density where transport density drops below Atlantic Water thickness times
0.04 m/s. Store separately the widths obtained at 0.02 and 0.08 m/s, preserving
their order and association. This is boundary-choice sensitivity, not an
uncertainty probability, confidence interval, observation range or annual range.
Do not infer that the two sensitivity cases are extrema over all thresholds.
Section C uses the SADCP-based offshore edge x=-11 km because coarse LADCP
spacing did not resolve the lateral decay. Do not apply that edge to A or B.

Tube 2 uses approximately 1.3 Sv transport conservation within 10%, with
successive equal distance increments from the core; section B is shelf-limited.
This tolerance is a transport constraint, not a width error. A1 and A2 have
the same source tube, not independent observations. B/C tube 2 is a constrained
Svalbard-branch interpretation; C1 includes flow before branch partitioning.
Never average the two tube definitions or sum their widths.

Atlantic Water support is property-defined: practical salinity >34.88 (the
source's corresponding rounded absolute-salinity criterion >35.05 g/kg),
conservative temperature >=2 C, limited by the seabed. The observed 45–475 m
water-mass depth span is not a constant width layer. The 100 dbar reference
pressure, 50–500 m comparison velocities, 500 m isobath path and 1 km/2 m
interpolation grid are separate contextual quantities. Retain the source's
10 km by 10 m smoothing and its sampling limitations.

The cruise was 12–21 August 2015; A/B/C occupations span five days, lasting
approximately 20/11/20 hours. Exact section dates are unextracted. Preserve
cruise context separately; do not assign those bounds to each width sample.
No monthly climatology, seasonal playback, whole-current width, annual extrema,
ranking, reconstructed width edges or state containment is admitted.

The approximately 0/86/171 km C/B/A coordinate follows the 500 m isobath for
the heat-budget calculation. It is a regional study coordinate, not the
whole-current length or a supplied geographic centerline.
