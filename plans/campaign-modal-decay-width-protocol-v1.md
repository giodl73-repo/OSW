# Campaign modal decay width · version 1

This convention covers a source's offshore modal decay estimate associated with a finite field campaign. It does not supply a paired velocity envelope or a recurring seasonal width.

1. Preserve the exact branch, region, vertical mode and coastal reference state. A nearshore branch is not its entire named current system.
2. Record the normalized offshore function, coefficient units and edge criterion separately. For Matsuyama (1990), Y/Y(coast) = exp(-gamma_1 y), gamma_1 = 1.3e-4 m^-1, and the adopted criterion is exp(-3), approximately 5%. The coefficient is a separation constant from a density-profile eigenvalue problem; it is not a fitted observed offshore velocity threshold.
3. Preserve the author's result (22 km) independently of the calculation from the printed coefficient (3/gamma_1/1000 = 23.076923... km). Preserve the approximate 20 km summary as context from the same estimate. The unresolved arithmetic discrepancy is neither an error bar nor a seasonal range. Do not replace the reported value silently or count its summary as another observation.
4. Keep campaign duration, individual station durations, current averaging periods and hydrographic cast date as different supports. Do not assign one homogeneous observation interval to the modal estimate where its inputs have different operators. `observed_period` stays null; campaign and operator dates remain queryable in the method context.
5. Summer 1980 is a finite campaign, not a recurring calendar. July and August sampling do not create monthly widths, annual extrema or playback frames.
6. A seasonal thermocline, instrument depths and water depths do not define a fixed measurement slab. Retain mode and layer language; keep fixed layer bounds unresolved.
7. Coast-to-isobath distance, along-isobath station spacing, speeds and strait dimensions are separate quantities. None supplies paired current boundaries or an uncertainty interval.
8. Preserve source caption/body longitude contradictions. A separate 1964–1985 hydrographic climatology cannot become the location or dates of the 1980 width. No geometry is admitted until this support is resolved and boundaries extracted.
9. Draw the mathematical decay diagram with an explicit model label, normalized amplitude and offshore-distance units. Display reported, summary and recalculated values separately; do not draw a route buffer or a width range between them.
10. Pin original bytes, acquisition receipt, protocol and full reviewed extraction. Python and native/WASM Rust reject changed support, erased method context and eligibility promotion even when query projections and receipts are edited together.

11. Preserve a printed differential-equation/sign conflict independently of the reported estimate. Matsuyama p161 Eq8 prints `d2Y_n/dy2 + gamma_n^2*Y_n = 0` but states `Y_n ~ exp(-gamma_n*y)` as its solution. For real positive gamma that expression does not satisfy the printed plus-sign equation. This is an editorial consistency finding, not an author-reported correction. Do not silently change the sign or claim the diagram validates the source derivation; it illustrates the separately published exponential expression.

Scientific admission, source equation/arithmetic discrepancies, compatible boundary occupations, numerical uncertainty, exact offshore edges and recurring seasonal variation remain open gates.
