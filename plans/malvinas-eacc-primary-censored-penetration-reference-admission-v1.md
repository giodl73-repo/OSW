using their own unchanged source configuration. Do not change a source operator,

## Source and selection

Use only retained malvinas-eacc-depth-sections-v1 data and the original v29
projection operator/addendum. Reproduce all original projections and unresolved
cases before deriving any reference. Verify manifest, raw section hashes,
transport ledger, units, instantaneous dates, native geometry, signed component,

The original criteria are the northward component, primary >0.1m/s, complete

January15 and July15,2022T00Z are instantaneous snapshots, not seasonal extrema.
Broad fixed longitude gates and interior maxima are not independently validated
current axes. No physical branch identity is established by this admission.

## Eligibility and two distinct censoring classes

Require the ORIGINAL primary selected layer to be surface-bounded at 0m and
the ORIGINAL selected maximum to be interior with finite horizontal neighbors.
Every native level from the surface through the bound must have a finite signed
component strictly above the original primary cutoff. Use the final contiguous
supported native depth as an inclusive >= lower bound, without interpolation.

1. Missing signed component: lower_status is
   censored_before_missing_or_dry_level; the next native level exists and its
   signed component is null. Stop at the last preceding supported level. Missing
   data does not establish the bed, a zero velocity or a lower current boundary.
2. Native vertical domain limit: lower_status is
   censored_at_sample_domain_edge; support reaches the final native level and
   there is no next native level. Retain the domain depth and level count. This
   is a domain-censored model component, not evidence of a current bottom at
   5000m, bathymetric depth, coherent branch identity or full-column transport.

Reject resolved lower contours, detached primary layers, section-edge maxima,
missing/weak support inside the claimed connected interval and absent selection.
Never replace a rejected primary axis or layer with a weaker sensitivity case.
No outcome-dependent thresholds, gates or windows. Audit both owners even if
one contributes no eligible new references. No expected count is a coverage gate.
