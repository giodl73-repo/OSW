## Source and scope

Schott and McCreary (2001), *The monsoon circulation of the Indian Ocean*,
Progress in Oceanography 51, 1–123, section 4.1.1, printed p.38, reports a
50–100 km width scale for the shallow northward northern coastal flow in its
March–May description. It overlies a southward undercurrent. The preceding
southern EACC extension and its 3–4 N turn-off do not define this width's edges.

One seasonal prose span is stored with months [3,4,5], null scalar and exact
occupation dates. This is not a seasonal mean section, three monthly values,
an annual range, an uncertainty interval or a whole-current envelope. Numerical
layer, boundary cutoff and geographic edges remain unextracted. Other months
remain unknown. Great Whirl and Southern Gyre scales do not supply this width.

without imposing a mean-field or per-month measurement convention. Python pins
