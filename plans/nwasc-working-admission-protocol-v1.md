## Identity

Admit `current:norwegian-atlantic-slope` as the Norwegian Atlantic Slope Current
(NwASC). Baumann et al. (2026), doi:10.5194/os-22-17-2026, identifies the
Atlantic-water flow along the Norwegian continental slope and its northern
bifurcation. The original PDF/XML already retained under CC BY4.0 support
identity and two static mean-core extents. It is distinct from the ambiguous
legacy Norwegian Current, Norwegian Coastal Current and western frontal branch.
The proposed Norwegian Atlantic Current parent remains proposed; do not invent
a canonical parent or make the slope current its alias.
