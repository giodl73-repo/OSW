# South Australian Current: reported model width

Source: Henry A. Miller (2006), *Modeling studies of the coastal/littoral
current system off Southern Australia*, Naval Postgraduate School dissertation.
The complete original is retained with its acquisition receipt. Public-release
and unlimited-distribution statements support retaining the original; OSW makes
no public-domain or open-license assertion.

Transcribe the approximate local width stated on printed p.49, tied to
Experiment 6, model day20 and the 130°E section in Figure4.20e (printed p.233).
Keep author-reported precision. Do not digitize boundaries or invent a velocity
cutoff from the figure. Treat annual climatological **forcing** as the experiment
configuration, not annual averaging of current widths. Model elapsed days have
no observed calendar date. The described surface-current scale has no fixed
measurement-depth bounds in the width statement.

This closes acquisition, source attribution and experiment-support gaps for
one local model-width report. It does not close whole-current physical width,
observed width, annual extrema, seasonal playback, length or geographic
containment gaps. Existing 2012 HYCOM component diagnostics retain their own
sources, layer and time support; they are not validated by this experiment.

Store one owner-bound source report, expose it through the existing Rust source
index, and show its numeric summary on the current's atlas card. Preserve all
existing width records, routes, seasonal data and scientific calculations.
The source-defined report stays separate from the existing measurement
inventory until a subsequent admission decision. No duplicate independent
measurement is inferred from the related Batteen and Miller (2009) paper.

Verification must bind original PDF, receipt, protocol, audit and owner. Reject
altered quantity, source association, model day or observation/annual-mean
promotion even when JSON hashes are recomputed. Verify the actual card, its
source-query link, text alternative and narrow-screen layout.
