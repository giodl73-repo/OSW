# Published Australian section transport: version 1

1. Use the unchanged 23-page final Wijeratne, Pattiaratchi and Proctor (2018)
   original from the University of Tasmania repository. Review original Table 1
   (PDF p7 / printed 3450), model methods and relevant section discussions.
2. Preserve all 35 Table 1(b) transport entries, including both core entries,
   full westward Flinders entries and four EAC footnote-a variants. Distinguish
   alternative/inclusive quantities; do not sum them or infer a closed budget.
3. Period for Table 1(b) is the 2000–2014 ozROMS hindcast. It has no data
   assimilation. These are published model statistics, not observed velocities,
   time series ingested by OSW, current footprints or annual dimensional margins.
4. Copy direction labels independently of magnitude. Keep Figure 3's clockwise
   sign convention separate from north/east/south/west table labels. Do not assign
   a global signed scalar to nonparallel sections or correct source directions.
5. Keep Table 1(b) plus/minus figures as source-reported dispersion with its
   statistical definition unresolved. Table 1(a) explicitly labels standard
   deviation. Neither becomes standard error, confidence interval, width error
   or a seasonal range. Do not derive probabilities or extrema from whiskers.
6. Preserve Table 1(a)'s six paired validation rows with observation/model mean,
   direction, explicitly reported standard deviation, RMSE, period and month
   count. Observation coverage is incomplete; model comparison means belong to
   those comparison periods, not automatically the full 15-year run. Exact
   matched observation/model monthly masks are not supplied here. M1 directions differ
   (observation W, simulation S). M6 says 18 months for Apr 2012–Aug 2013
   (17 inclusive calendar months). Retain both; do not repair or invent dates.
7. Preserve source core-layer labels. Only eastern Table 1(b) explicitly supplies
   upper 2000 m bounds; western/southern/northern surface/subsurface cores have
   no fixed bounds assigned from Figure 1's separate 0–300 / 300–1000 m maps.
8. Section latitude/longitude labels supply one coordinate only. No endpoints,
   geographic width, physical containment/intersection, or current-axis location
   is inferred. Map guides represent partial coordinate context, not sections.
9. Assign canonical owners only for existing clearly corresponding current
   identities and source components. Holloway, Flinders and Leeuwin Undercurrent
   source labels remain unassigned where the ledger has no matching identity.
   Do not merge them into the western/southern Australian family or Leeuwin.
10. Source-reported seasonal transport ranges and peak months are separate from
    numeric monthly values and dimensional margins. No graph digitization,
    monthly interpolation, year animation or recurring width phases admitted.
11. Show a common native Rust scene in query, atlas and evidence pages, with
    original source rows, separate statistic roles, units (Sv = million m³/s),
    sampling limits and source inspection. Frozen complete collections protect
    scientific values against coherent receipt/owner edits in native and WASM.

No raw ozROMS output or IMOS mooring records are reprocessed here. The advertised
raw-model THREDDS endpoint timed out on 2026-10-09. Original copyright is retained;
the original PDF is ignored and no publisher figures are republished.
