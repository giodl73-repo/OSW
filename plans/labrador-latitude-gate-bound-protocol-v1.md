## Original and scope

Thompson et al. (2009), doi:10.1029/2008JC004859, section 4.2 paragraph 29,
PDF pages 6–7, describes regional shelf/slope flow between Hudson Strait at
60°N and the tail of Grand Bank at 43°N. Onshore branches and Flemish Cap
bifurcation differ. It does not supply a dated connected axis, common fixed
measurement layer, paired endpoints or a numeric whole-current length.

The institutional original is 572833 bytes, SHA-256

It is retained privately, not redistributed. The existing public custody

transcription and exact visual-review scope. Default arithmetic reproduction
uses those pinned factual inputs offline. Optional `original --original-path`
verifies exact local original custody; it is not a new network test gate.

## Operator and consistency

Reuse the frozen North Atlantic WGS84 meridional calculation without editing
it. Compute the shortest latitude separation from 43°N to 60°N on an abstract
common longitude of zero; this longitude is never a current location. Keep
six decimal kilometres for arithmetic readback and round down to a 100 km
multiple for the conditional nominal geographic floor.

Shift each latitude independently by −0.5°, 0°, +0.5° and retain all nine
scenarios. This is the same editorial sensitivity convention used for the
North Atlantic bound. It is not source-reported position error, statistical
uncertainty, a confidence interval, annual extrema or seasonal variability.
The central rounded floor need not be below every editorial scenario. No
guaranteed physical minimum or continuous axis is established by prose gates.
