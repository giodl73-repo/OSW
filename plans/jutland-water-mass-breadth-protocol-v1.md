# Jutland regional water-mass breadth convention v1

This is an editorial extraction from Skov, Mortensen and Tuhuteru (2019),
prepared by DHI for the Danish Energy Agency. Independent scientific admission
remains pending. The unchanged original is retained locally and excluded from Git.

1. Transcribe the 10–20 km span in section 2.3, printed p15 / PDF p27, as the
   reported breadth of a **surface water mass** from Henne Strand north of Horns
   Rev along the coast to Skagen. It is not a velocity-core measurement.
2. Preserve both endpoints without inventing a midpoint, probability interval,
   numerical uncertainty, temporal extrema or whole-current representative value.
   The original describes this water mass as Jutland Current / Continental Coastal
   Water, citing Nielsen (1999). The underlying source and boundary method remain
   unreviewed. This extraction uses the report's label, without resolving the
   North/South Jutland labels in the separate naming audit.
3. Almost 100 km offshore traceability and a core typically within 40 km of the
   Wadden Sea are coast-distance context at another location, not paired width
   edges or endpoints of this span. Do not combine or subtract these quantities.
4. The roughly 40 km zone in section 3.1.1.1, printed p23 / PDF p35, describes
   high bird-habitat suitability near Horns Rev. Keep it separate from current
   width. Its 15–30 m depth zone is bathymetry, not a fixed current layer.
   The nearby 20 km prose refers to a different geographic reach starting at
   Ringkøbing Fjord; do not select it as a preferred value or dated sample.
5. Figure 6 (PDF p28) shows DHI modeled fields for December 2018. This date is
   not support for the undated breadth statement. Bird-density seasonality,
   salinity/temperature variation and publication year do not supply seasonal
   width observations. Width occupation dates and fixed layer bounds stay null.
6. No transect endpoints, geographic current edges or salinity threshold were
   extracted. Show the span as a textual description with a source link; do not
   attach a band to the editorial route, infer a full-width polygon, rank it,
   animate it, or inherit it to Norwegian Coastal Current or North/South aliases.
7. Pin the original PDF, acquisition receipt, protocol and complete audit row.
   Validate record identity independently of editable context in Python and
   native/WASM Rust, including context deletion and owner reassignment.
