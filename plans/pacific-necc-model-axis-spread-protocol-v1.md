# Published model axis-distance scale: measurement supplement

Continue the current measurement work in draft PR #83. No new PR or merge. Source values and figures were read before this source-extraction declaration; no blinded analysis or preregistration is claimed.

## Meaning and allowed transcription

Chen, Qiu, Du, Chen and Qi (2016), doi:10.1002/2016JC012190, report an approximate change from 100 to 130 km for the western Pacific NECC axis width scale in their 1960-2014 OFES analysis. Transcribe those two kilometre summaries separately. They are not a full jet width, annual minimum/maximum, confidence interval, observed climatology or exact 1960/2014 endpoints. Their original Figure 8 time series has a latitude-degree axis and excursions outside the reported summary states. Do not turn the two values into a bounding interval or digitize a time series implicitly.

Equations 1-3 define a transport-weighted axis position and mean absolute distance from it. The weight is zonal velocity integrated over 0-150 m, set to zero when westward. It is not vector speed, RMS distance, a Gaussian standard deviation, half-maximum crossing or paired-edge width. Do not multiply the reported scale by two or four to manufacture full width. At zero eastward transport the normalized scale is undefined, not zero.

Latitude integration uses 0-10 N, followed by zonal averaging over 135-155 E and a 13-month running-mean filter for Figure 8. Retain these operator, spatial and temporal supports. Figure 8's latitude-degree values are not independently converted by OSW; the 100/130 km numbers come directly from the authors' conclusion. Numerical conversion, filtering endpoints and exact dates of the two summary states remain unextracted.

## Provenance and restrictions

Retain the exact university-hosted original locally with source URL, retrieval time, bytes, SHA256 and producer receipt. Copyright is not a redistribution license: keep the PDF and its rendered page images outside Git. Default source reconstruction remains offline with the pinned local paper. New public figures must be OSW scalar diagrams, with full attribution and no copied source panel.

The source uses OFES with 0.1-degree horizontal grid, 54 vertical levels and NCEP-NCAR forcing. This is a model diagnostic. The paper's comparisons with sea-level records and repeat hydrography do not give independent observations of a continuous 55-year width record. Its alternative wind-forcing comparison cautions against treating long-term model axis changes as a uniquely verified observational trend.

Do not join the western-Pacific model scale to the 140 W, 2013 OSCAR component spans. Retain that candidate and its physical-admission gates. No current-edge coordinates, mapped bands, geographic footprints, state intersections, annual ranges or seasonal playback are admitted from the two reported model states. Layer bounds describe velocity-weight integration, not measured physical edges throughout the layer.
