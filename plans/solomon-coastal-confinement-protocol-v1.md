# Coastal confinement descriptions — version 1

1. Preserve the author's one-sided relation to the coast. A current lying within
   40 or 100 km of a coast does not establish two velocity boundaries or a full
   width. Store a reference confinement scale; keep representative width, range,
   uncertainty and boundary coordinates unknown.
2. Keep the source's SICU model result separate from its NICU citation of Butt
   and Lindstrom (1994). Reviewing Melet et al. (2010) does not constitute review
   of the earlier cruise paper or independently corroborate its measurements.
3. SICU model context: nested NEMO/OPA 1/12 degree simulation, 46 z levels,
   1986–2004 analyzed climatology after a 1984–1985 adjustment. This period is
   not a current-width occupation interval. Mean thermocline sigma 24.0–26.5
   varies in depth; roughly 100–400 m is not a fixed width sampling slab.
4. Core depth/speed, grid spacing, section longitude gates and transport
   standard deviations do not define width edges, width uncertainty or width
   seasonality. NICU 235 m/60 cm/s is cited observational core context; model
   25.5 sigma/65 cm/s and 154.5 E integration gate are different supports.
5. The model's monthly transports and three-month flow anomalies are not monthly
   widths. No seasonal width range, extrema, ranked dimension or playback is
   admitted. No map buffer or physical OSW-state intersection is derived.
6. Figures 1, 4, 9 and 12 may establish study geography and section meaning;
   inspecting them does not digitize paired edges or a current axis. Existing
   editorial routes remain geographic candidates, not source-diagnosed geometry.
7. Preserve both current identities; do not inherit a gateway width or a
   neighboring current's scale. Keep unknowns and remaining primary-method gates
   visible in cards, inspector, dashboard and queries.
