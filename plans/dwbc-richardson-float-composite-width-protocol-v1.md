# DWBC upper-core float-composite width

1. Use the complete final Richardson and Schmitz (1993) original. Preserve the
   approximate 100 km width between zero-velocity points in the composite
   along-boundary velocity profile, not a threshold of individual float speeds.
2. Coordinates are distance seaward of the source 1800 m isobath and velocity
   rotated parallel/normal to that contour. Ten-kilometre bins are averaging
   support, not numerical width error. No geographic current edges extracted.
3. Floats were at nominal 1800 m; active depth control and temperature/pressure
   transmission failed. Source estimates about 230 m sinking over 21 months.
   Do not assign a fixed 1800 m layer or borrow 900-2800 m transport bounds.
4. Preserve all four Table 2 rows as auxiliary source summaries. The composite
   overlaps three historical subsets; repeated 100 km entries do not establish
   independently tracked edges or an invariant current width. No annual extrema,
   recurring seasons, monthly interpolation or temporal width playback admitted.
5. Preserve source labels and discrepancies: Table 2 domain 2 S-12 N west of
   43 W differs from section 3.2's 0-7 N / 12-month description. Do not reconcile
   them by inventing exact width dates. Table 2 subset counts total 489 rather
   than composite 500; float counts overlap and are not additive.
6. Keep peak individual speeds separate from maximum 10 km bin averages and
   their standard errors. Transport per unit depth is in 10^3 m2/s, not Sv.
   Source errors for velocity/transport do not provide width uncertainty.
7. Table 2 composite transport per unit depth is rounded 14 +/-3; prose gives
   13.8, and an alternative three-subset standard error about 4. Preserve those
   roles. Total volume transport 15 Sv is rounded from a 14.7 Sv calculation
   coupling floats with an independent mooring and an assumed elliptical shape
   over 900-2800 m. No total Sv values inferred for the three subsets.
   First-subset offshore sampling gap 90-130 km is not a width range; source
   warns that its width and transport could be larger than reported.
8. Source reports no floats in the boundary current April-December 1989 and
   cautions that time-variation conclusions are subjective with sparse samples.
   This gap is not zero current or absence of the DWBC. No observed daily velocity
   series, geographic footprint, along-current length or physical state passage
   is reconstructed. Existing North Atlantic reference reach is separate.
9. Render width plus source subset diagnostics from one checked Rust scene in
   query, atlas and evidence pages. Label velocity/transport whiskers as their
   reported standard errors, never width uncertainty or confidence intervals.
   Source Figure 2 map bounds may frame the OSW basemap as study geography only;
   a viewport is not a current footprint, trajectory or measured width edge.
