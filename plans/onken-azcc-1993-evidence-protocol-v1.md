# Azores Countercurrent: original-source evidence rules

Review Onken (1993), The Azores Countercurrent, JPO23,1638-1646,
doi:10.1175/1520-0485(1993)023<1638:TAC>2.0.CO;2. Retain the ordinary
public GEOMAR PDF privately and version its acquisition and rendering receipts.
All nine original pages must be rendered and individually inspected.

Keep these quantities separate:

- The linear, steady Sverdrup model uses a2x2-degree grid and vertically
  integrated flow. Its36-40N westward band is a model result, not a measured
  surface width or a fixed-depth current corridor.
- The discussion's17-55W interval describes several evidence locations from
  multiple periods and methods. These are neither physical endpoints nor a
  sampled continuous current axis. Do not turn the38-degree longitudinal span
  into a kilometre length, a lower bound, a route or a length ranking.
- The approximately200km width is Onken's interpretation of historical
  sections discussed through Worthington1962/1976. Treat it as a dependent,
  cited regional summary. Do not label it a new independent observation,
  seasonal extreme, confidence interval or fixed-layer width.
- The NEADS2 record at38N17W, nominal750m,533days and mean westward zonal
  component1.2cm/s is a cited record from Gould1983. Dates and the original
  measurement source remain unreviewed. The40N45W,500-600m indication is
  explicitly not statistically significant and must remain separate.
- Layer supports differ: dynamic height at600dbar, transport integrated over
 0-800m, a comparison restricted to upper1000dbar, and individual mooring
 depths cannot be merged into one representative layer or thickness.

Prepare a compact evidence-location and cited-width graphic. Its longitude
axis must state that it is evidence support, with no connected geographic
current path. Preserve unknown whole-current length, annual width range,
numerical width uncertainty, observation dates and diagnosed current edges.
No figure coordinates are digitized and no seasonal playback is enabled.
