Declared2026-10-22, after inspecting the primary publisher HTML; not blinded.
Source: Schonau and Rudnick2015, DOI10.1002/2014JC010595, section3.1 and Figure6.
This is factual source transcription, not a new estimator or field reproduction.

1. Preserve two different eastward undercurrent jets at134.3E, mean cores9.6N
   and13.1N. Assign to the existing Pacific North Equatorial Undercurrent identity;
   neither is the overlying westward North Equatorial Current.
2. Retain author-reported width ranges100-350km and0-200km separately.
   Do not compute midpoints, means, errors or a combined0-350km envelope.
   The second source lower bound is explicitly zero, not a substituted missing
   value. No exact dated zero-width sample or annual disappearance is inferred.
3. Metric: meridional extent of eastward absolute zonal geostrophic velocity,
   in the source's26.7-27.3kg/m3 potential-density averaging context.
   Preserve the source density notation; do not translate it to metre depths,
   fixed vertical thickness or a flow-normal estimator.
4. June2009-January2014 is the study window, not an extrema-date pair, annual
   cycle or continuously sampled time series. Roughly6km profile separation
   and80km objective-mapping length are different sampling/analysis scales;
   neither is a width confidence interval. Sections take months, not instants.
5. The broader southern jet can have two cores and possible combination above
   300km. Preserve that ambiguity. Northern jet surface-current connections
   can involve eddies. Do not infer independently separated edges at all times.
6. Core means and excursions are locators, not width boundaries. Admit no new
   map geometry, occupied polygon, state passage, width buffer or length rank.
   No seasonal samples, interpolation or playback are enabled by these ranges.
7. Primary HTML was inspected through the research browser on2026-10-22.
   Direct original HTML/PDF archival fetch returned403. Do not invent an
   original-file checksum, claim a downloaded PDF or redistribute publisher

8. Extend the prior frozen ASAR export with exactly two records and one source.
   Keep all previous widths, measurements and lengths byte/row equivalent.
   Validate source semantics and joins in Rust and actual native/WASM queries.
   Working editorial admission does not mean official scientific approval.
