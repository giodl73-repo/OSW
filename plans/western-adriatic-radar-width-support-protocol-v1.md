## Operators and scope

1. Section 5 paragraph 30, PDF6-7, reports 40 km off Ravenna and 50 km off
   Pesaro, from mean alongshore-flow profiles on cross-shore sections AA' and
   BB' in Figures 13-14 (PDF14). Preserve two local mean values. They are not
   the endpoints of a 40-50 km seasonal, annual or uncertainty interval.
2. Deployment is October 2002 to October 2004, at month precision. Figure 2
   (PDF3) shows site-specific gaps, including no early Ravenna record. Exact
   averaging weights, sample counts and identical valid times for the two
   sections are unresolved. Keep this as study-period context; do not invent
   exact days or a common continuous width occupation interval.
3. Measurements are HFR surface velocities; the approximately 1 m effective
   measurement depth in paragraph 13 is not a hard 0-1 m slab. The compared
   unstratified tidal model is a separate product and lacks the WAC mesoscale
   circulation. These width values are not model predictions or deep widths.
4. Paragraphs 8-12: 16 MHz radar, 1.5 km range resolution, 5 km Cartesian
   vector grid and 5 km search radius. Neither 1.5 nor 5 km is numerical width
   uncertainty. About 80% time with data is not uniform coverage everywhere.
   Paragraph 47 GDOP rejection/interpolation is an observation processing
   rule, not a current-edge threshold. Raw velocity fields are not recovered.
5. Figure 14 shows mean alongshore velocity versus distance from shore. The
   author reports rounded widths. Figure 15 averages coast to distance of no
   mean flow; retain this contextual boundary convention, without claiming
   digitized exact zero-crossings or geographic edges. Radar site coordinates
   are instrument locations, not transect endpoints or current boundaries.
6. Figure 13 confidence ellipses describe mean velocity, not width confidence.
   The 20 km discrepancy band in paragraph 26 and the abstract's 10-20 km
   strip concern tidal-model errors, not current width or a width range.
   Peak flow positions 20/10 km offshore and maxima 6/12 cm/s are separate
   quantities, as are estimated transports 0.04/0.08 Sv with stratification
   neglected. None gives a width threshold or uncertainty.
7. Paragraph 31/Figure 15 show velocity strengthening in fall/winter and
   reduction/reversal in spring/summer. The 3-day and 30-day running medians
   filter velocity time series, not width samples. No numerical seasonal
   widths or monthly width playback follow. Figure 14 defines southeastward
   positive; Figure 15 defines southeastward negative. Preserve the different
   source sign conventions without merging the graphs or inventing a fix.

## Admission

Both records are editorial local mean descriptions, stored under regional
summary admission rules. They cannot establish full-current width, rankings,
annual extrema, compatible seasonal series, mapped edge bands or state joins.
Selected PDF pages 1,2,3,6,7,14,15 inspected; no full-paper review or figure
digitization claimed. Bind original, acquisition, protocol, audit and full
records across Python and compiled Rust/WASM. Require raw fields, reproducible
paired boundaries, compatible repeat support and independent scientific review
before stronger admission. Previous current records and route geometry remain.
