# Baffin regional width batch

2026-10-09. Based on Humboldt draft PR63. Two editorial regional summaries
from Fissel, Lemon and Birch (1982), Arctic 35(1), 180-200,
doi:10.14430/arctic2318, retained through the DFO institutional original.

| Support | Width | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| East Devon near-surface flow | About 25 km | Printed p198 / PDF19 |
| Lancaster Sound intrusion | 10-30 km average regional span | Printed p198 / PDF19 |

These have different spatial supports. The Lancaster passage's possible
factor-of-two widening is along the excursion, not seasonal change. Offshore
center positions, sampled drifter envelopes and eddy diameters remain separate
quantities. No midpoint or combined range is inferred. Summer 1978/1979 and
winter current-meter records do not define exact width occupation dates.
Instrument depths and the 300 dbar dynamic-topography reference are not fixed
width layers. Unusually severe ice in both summers limits extrapolation.

Selected original pages 3,4,5,10,19,20 were visually inspected. No full-paper
review, raw-field reconstruction or boundary digitization is claimed. The
2,154,964-byte, 21-page source is unchanged and SHA256-pinned. Redistribution
rights are unestablished, so the PDF stays ignored; factual extraction and
original OSW annotations are distributed.
