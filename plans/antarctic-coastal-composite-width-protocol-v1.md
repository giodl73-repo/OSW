# Antarctic Coastal Current composite sections

1. Use the final Schubert et al. (2021) article, doi:10.5194/tc-15-4179-2021.
   Preserve Table 2 widths as seven separate source-computed composite sections
   in their published east-to-west order. Spatial differences are not an annual
   width range or seven consecutive dates.
2. Define shoreward support as the coastline or ice-shelf face. The offshore
   edge is where depth-integrated velocity reaches 15% of its value at the
   center identified through the maximum gradient of net transport.
3. Geostrophic velocity is referenced to a 400 m level of no motion. The
   transport integration runs from the surface to the variable 34.4 psu
   isohaline. Do not treat 400 m as a fixed current layer or Table 2 depth
   ranges as numerical uncertainty in width.
4. Preserve Table 1 cross-shelf section lengths, averaging-bin sizes and
   summer/winter/total profile counts. Section length is not along-current
   length or current width. Latitude spans cannot be converted as if every
   transect were meridional.
5. Preserve the detailed, disjoint sampling-period descriptions from section
   2.1. Composites combine years and seasons; no exact section width dates,
   monthly series, or calendar interpolation are extracted. Table 1 shows
   summer dominance for sections 1 and 6. Section 2.3 prose singles out only
   section 1; flag section 6's discrepancy instead of changing the counts.
6. Table 2 transports are signed source estimates in Sv, negative westward.
   Prose mean/peak velocities are geostrophic diagnostics in m/s, with the
   authors' caution about the spatial average retained. Bootstrap velocity
   and transport errors cannot supply missing width uncertainty.
7. No geographic edge coordinates, current footprint, simultaneous connecting
   axis, physical OSW state passage, whole-current width or canonical length
   is inferred. The regional coastal current stays distinct from the Antarctic
   Slope Current. No new naming equivalence is admitted.
8. Display the credited source Figure 3 as a profile-location map. Extract its
   embedded raster without recoloring, resampling or added markings. Its
   colored profile distributions are not current outlines. Verify image bytes
   before displaying it. Numerical graphics and row selections come from the
   same checked Rust scene in atlas, seasonal inspector and query UI.

Selected original pages 1, 4, 5, 11, 12 and 13 were visually inspected. The
unchanged CC BY 4.0 original and attributed figure are shipped. No MEOP raw
profiles were ingested, no geographic figure digitization was performed and
the hydrographic calculation was not independently reproduced.
