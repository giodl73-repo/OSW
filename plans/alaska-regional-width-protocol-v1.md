# Alaska regional width convention v1

2026-10-08. Supplement to unchanged general width protocol v1.21.

Retain the approximate 300 km breadth reported for the Alaska Current in
Weingartner et al. (2002), Physical Setting, printed p49, with sentence
continuation on p50. Use existing `regional_summary`,
`published_regional_summary` and `author_reported_current_width` conventions.
The eastern Gulf of Alaska boundary current is distinct from the Alaskan
Stream and the inner-shelf Alaska Coastal Current.

This is an approximate regional background point, not a field-survey width,
flow-normal section with paired coordinates, or whole-current representative
dimension. Numerical uncertainty, range, fixed layer and observation dates
remain null. General year-round persistence does not create twelve monthly
width samples. The GLOBEC program's 1997/1998 start, 2001 cruises, 2002 plans
and 40 m drifter depth do not define this width's dates or layer. Figure 1's
wind and discharge climatology periods do not define a width climatology.

Do not turn the neighboring Alaskan Stream's approximate 100 km into a range
endpoint, assign the Alaska Coastal Current's coastal confinement as this
width, or use shelf breadth as current breadth. No figure digitization, route
buffer, occupancy polygon or state intersection follows from the number.
The Reed and Schumacher (1986) citation at the sentence's end is retained
with its unresolved attribution to the Alaska breadth and unreviewed method.

Display a single point labeled approximate, without range caps or uncertainty
whiskers; give layer, temporal and boundary unknowns in readable text. Bind
the complete record to original, acquisition, protocol and audit receipts
in Python and compiled Rust. Ranking, annual extrema, full-width inference
and seasonal playback remain false. Independent scientific admission and
compatible boundary/repeat evidence remain required. Retain the unchanged
publisher original with CC BY 4.0 attribution and license link.
