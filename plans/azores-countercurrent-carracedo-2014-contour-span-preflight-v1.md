## Fixed attempted cases

1. Read all five panels: annual, winter, spring, summer, autumn, in source
   order. Seasons are WOA09 January–March, April–June, July–September and
   October–December, as already verified in the original methods. Annual
   mean is separate; do not assign it a month or use it as an annual extremum.
2. Attempt the explicitly labelled **negative0.5cm/s** contour at each of
   **800,1200,1600 source-plot metres**, yielding15 attempts. The plot labels
   its vertical axis metres; do not substitute pressure dbar or imply a
   pressure/depth conversion. The depths sample upper/intermediate portions
   of the plotted counterflow and are fixed before quantitative readings.
   No threshold/depth substitution if a case fails.
3. Work only on the western meridional section at19.5W. The source associates
   westward counterflow north of the Azores Current with ACC, and integrates
   its transport over36–40N/first1800dbar (prose on printed122). That is a
   source naming/transport context, not a paired-boundary rule or depth-metre
   definition. For these figure readings require a clearly identifiable
   connected negative0.5cm/s lobe in the36–40N context, with both same-depth
   crossings identifiable inside the western section. Do not bridge panels,
   section seams, masked bottom, zero contours or different negative lobes.
4. If multiple candidate lobes/labels are ambiguous, if the selected lobe
   extends beyond the named latitude context, or if either side is absent,
   unreadable, clipped or confused with an annotation, retain a null span and
   explicit reason. Do not select the widest lobe, close at a window edge,
   extrapolate, double one side or equate a transport gate with a contour.
5. Calibrate the western-section horizontal axis using at least two verified
   labelled latitude ticks. Calibrate each panel's vertical source-depth axis
   separately. Preserve native pixels, tick positions, residuals and finite
   crossing bounds covering stroke thickness plus at least one native pixel.
   Use the same ordinate on both sides. Retain sign and contour-label evidence.
6. Compute WGS84 meridional separation of the two interpolated source
   coordinates at19.5W. Report an approximate component-contour span rounded
   to10km. Propagate plot-reading bounds only as a graphic allowance, separately
   from unknown velocity/model/sampling errors. It is not a confidence interval.
