# Qualitative current calendars v1

Calendar claims are source interpretations, not dated observations, sampled
speed series, width ranges or occupied geometry. Preserve overlapping claims
and their reference states. Never turn an anomaly direction into absolute flow,
an indirect property indication into a measured axis, or a persistence endpoint
qualified by “at least” into a hard disappearance date.

Months use the Gregorian calendar without choosing a representative year.
Each month indexes every applicable source claim. Repeated claims across months
are one claim, not independent samples. Development, peak, persistence,
environmental transition and indirect indications remain distinct categories.
No numeric strength scale, interpolation, annual dimension extrema or seasonal
route morphing follows from these categories.

Keep source operators and their periods separate. A harmonic climatology is
not a sequence of dated occupations. Map context must retain the original route
source and evidence class; selecting a month cannot alter a static route.
