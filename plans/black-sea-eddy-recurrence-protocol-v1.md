# Named eddy recurrence protocol v1

Regional recurrence names are not individual tracked eddies. Store a stable
existing identity, source passage, source edition/hash, and publication citation.
The present source is an openly licensed 2011 synthesis, inspected as original
text and rendered printed page 633. Its quantitative paragraph references
Korotaev et al. (2003); no averaging window is assigned from a nearby dated map.

1. Preserve average presence days per year separately from per-event lifetime.
   Multiple events may contribute to presence; do not divide these estimates to
   invent a count of events or a probability at an arbitrary date.
2. Retain source units and qualifiers (mean versus typical). A month is not an
   exact number of days; narrative start/end seasons do not define exact dates.
3. Unknown event lifetime, observation window, detection threshold and numerical
   uncertainty stay null. Seasonal intensity does not determine eddy width.
4. Reuse existing regional IDs only after exact identity correspondence. Shared
   source sentences can support distinct named regions, not independent samples.
5. No monthly occupancy vector, animation, annual extrema, individual identity,
   geographic footprint, NASA generation match or physical state join follows.
6. Display presence on a labeled 0–365 day comparison scale. It is a descriptive
   average-year scale, not a claim that the sampling year had 365 days. Lifetime
   remains separately labeled text in its original unit and statistic.
7. Pin the complete manual extraction, protocol and generator; validate the
   collection identically in native Rust and shipped WASM. Changed receipt,
   values, identity or admission flags require renewed extraction.

Independent scientific admission and protected publication remain separate gates.
