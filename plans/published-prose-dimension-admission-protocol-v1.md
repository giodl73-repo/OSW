# Published prose dimension admission, version 1

Apply the existing length and width rules to numeric statements readable in a
primary publication's full text. A paper's original HTML is source text;
acquiring its PDF is not a prerequisite for a value explicitly stated in prose.
Original figure review remains required for figure-dependent extraction,
digitization or reconstructed geometry. This rule does not lift such gates.

1. Fix the accepted current/branch ID and verify source identity, citation and
   full-text passage locator. Record access mode and inspection date. Do not
   invent an original-file checksum or redistribution license.
2. Retain author precision and approximation. Identify whether the passage
   reports the authors' observations or a contextual estimate citing earlier
   literature. Repetition in an abstract or institutional record is not
   independent corroboration.
3. Length admission requires an explicitly named flow and an along-current or
   branch-extent statement, with its source-defined start/end convention.
   Retain unspecified coordinates, layer, date and error as null. A published
   approximate extent may join the qualified reported-length ranking; it is
   not a measured axis or a dated current footprint.
4. Width admission retains the reported region, survey/month and feature.
   Preserve a near-surface description without assigning a fixed width layer.
   A nearby velocity averaging layer is method context, not automatically the
   width layer. Missing edge coordinates and numerical cutoff stay unspecified.
5. Do not transfer a transport-integration contour, velocity uncertainty,
   offshore core position or current-core speed into width boundaries/errors.
   Do not turn one survey summary into multiple independent samples.
6. Width records are scoped editorial evidence, not whole-current widths,
   annual means/extrema, confidence intervals or width-ranking candidates.
   Never multiply separate length/width reports into area or buffer a route.
7. A dated survey width can be inspected as one non-playable state. Hide the
   geographic width bar and retain unknown annual ranges and geometry. A
   reported branch length can coexist with a pending route-construction task.
8. Record a source-specific admission and separate eligibility for each
   quantity. Keep the historical release unchanged; export working records,
   exact queries, source review and qualified visual summaries. Test mutation,
   duplication/erasure, source reassignment and promotion to physical/seasonal
   claims. Name admission alone cannot authorize these measurement records.

First application: Nauw et al. (2008), doi:10.1029/2007JC004639, southern East
Madagascar Current. Introduction paragraph2 reports approximately650km from
about20S to Cap Sainte Marie near25deg30minS, citing earlier work. Results3.1
paragraph17 reports approximately120km near-surface width over the April2001
T5–T8 survey. Upper100m VMADCP mean velocity is context; transport zero-velocity
contours and the 5C isotherm are not adopted as width definitions.
