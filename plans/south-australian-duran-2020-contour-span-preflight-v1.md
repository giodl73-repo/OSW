# South Australian model-contour span preflight v1

Declared before inspecting Figure5's raster or extracting contour crossings.
This is a feasibility study, not an admitted measurement.

1. Use the retained Duran et al.2020 original, SHA256
   `e110e867b50df35706d4786ea493d25b82cd03733c683725324db856bfeb401e`.
2. Inspect only the Cape Carnot section in Figure5 for the initial result.
   This location falls within the eastern Great Australian Bight regional
   South Australian Current scope. Do not substitute a stronger-looking
   section if this section has no resolved span.
3. Define the operator as meridional separation of the two crossings of the
   published positive eastward4 cm/s contour at100 m depth in the long-term
   mean MOM01 section. It is an OSW-selected component threshold, not a
   publisher-reported width, velocity-normal breadth or whole-current width.
4. Both contour crossings must be visibly resolved and associated with the
   same shelf-break jet. Missing, clipped, masked, ambiguous or coast-intersected
   crossings remain unresolved. Never use a control-volume line or bathymetric
   boundary as a substitute. Do not bridge gaps, double one-sided extent or
   change the threshold after seeing the profile.
5. Calibrate latitude and depth against plotted axis ticks. Record raw pixel
   locations and a reading bracket for line thickness plus one raster pixel.
   Convert latitude bounds with the WGS84 inverse geodesic along a meridian;
   longitude is irrelevant to that calculation and is not an inferred section
   location. Display to the nearest10 km. Plot-reading allowance is separate
   from unresolved model error and native0.1degree grid support.
6. Keep the seven model-year averaging support under repeated climatological
   forcing separate from calendar observations, CARS and2012HYCOM. Calendar
   observation dates, seasonal evolution, physical width error and annual
   extrema remain unknown.
7. No mapped paired edges without source-supported section coordinates. No
   route buffer, full-depth slab, current footprint or width ranking.
8. Before admission, independently recheck the original panel labels,
   threshold, depth, source period and association; review resolution limits
   and uncertainty type. If feasibility fails, retain the reason rather than
   fabricating a numeric width.
