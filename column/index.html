HOW TO READ IT

The address stays still. The ocean moves through it.

01

Reference

One declared province and one declared half-open depth interval identify an address class; occupancy still requires bathymetry.

02

Truncation

Bathymetry ends a local column. A 90 m shelf contains only the epipelagic band.

03

Overlay

Mixed layers, gradients, water masses, and bottom influence follow independent evidence.

04

Unknown

Missing or invalid water remains unclassified; it is never filled merely to complete the picture.
