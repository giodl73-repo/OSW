Evidence boundary: the province identity is a reference label. Selecting a province × band composes an address class; without measured bathymetry it does not establish that the combination contains wet volume. The three seabeds and physical overlays are teaching examples—not local bathymetry, observations, detected regimes, heat content, or transport.

Data identity and next evidence step

The 56 names come from OSW's classic province reference directory. Exact interval semantics come from the edition-1 JSON contract. Both source checksums are embedded in the generated browser bundle.

Real province-specific occupancy requires a versioned bathymetry grid, exact horizontal geometry, cell bounds, masks, and a reproducible intersection. GEBCO remains the candidate source; this stage does not download or imply it.

HOW TO READ IT

The address stays still. The ocean moves through it.

01

Reference

One declared province and one declared half-open depth interval identify an address class; occupancy still requires bathymetry.

02

Truncation

Bathymetry ends a local column. A 90 m shelf contains only the epipelagic band.

03

Overlay

Mixed layers, gradients, water masses, and bottom influence follow independent evidence.

04

Unknown

Missing or invalid water remains unclassified; it is never filled merely to complete the picture.
