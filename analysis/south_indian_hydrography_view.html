South Indian Current: paired source profiles

Model source investigation · 2013 · 80°E. No named-current width or axis has been established here. Eastward flow may include the nearby Antarctic Circumpolar Current (ACC). These profiles help assess that distinction.

Compare velocity with salinity and in-situ temperature from the same valid daily samples. Two native depths provide context; they are not an upper-1,000 m average. The neighboring meridians are closely spaced model sensitivities, not independent observations. One year's monthly samples do not establish a seasonal climatology.

Source and measurement rules

HYCOM GOFS 3.1, GLBv0.08 expt 53.X, 2013; exact daily 00:00 UTC samples. August 14 and December 25 are absent in the source. Every monthly cell requires common valid values for all four fields on at least 80% of calendar days. Missing cells are not filled. Values are total model velocities, not the deep-referenced geostrophic velocities in the historical paper.
