Top finding and cross-role consensus: CURRENT, SOUNDER and CHART require the
reference geometry, source support and reported quantity to remain distinct.
Supported-domain brackets cannot silently become coastline-based full widths.

## Three amendments applied

1. Keep conditional inshore coverage separate from native-scale status, and use
   only known supported model span to assess the source-scale gate.
2. Preserve every case/fraction/quantity slot, original regional flags and source
   status; verify actual5/10km spacing and retain all unresolved outcomes.
3. Add explicit unverified association/resolution/admission flags and correct the
   synthetic plot's missing-data hatching and curve clipping through visual QA.

## Conditions before original data

Implement a separate cloud adapter with exact source/method custody, verified
Earth-frame/geodesic mapping and whole-path native-cell scale. Handle original
unsupported peaks without selecting convenient replacements. Generate traceable
actual-data figures/textual evidence, complete the new dependency freeze and
request, then run only one cloud case. Do not call the completed old producers
or alter the62 executed paths. Independent source/geographic validation remains
required before physical measurement admission or generalization.
