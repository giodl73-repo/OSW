Top finding: the portable site is a useful, isolated review surface, but the
scientific claims and broader public site have not passed their release gates.
CURRENT and LOGBOOK agree that a passing browser test cannot substitute for
claim review and publication authority. CHART and BEACON agree that a state
locator must not be read as a measured current crossing.

Three amendments:

1. Add a reviewed geographic ground layer and tighten state summary wording.
2. Conduct a human accessibility and scientific claim review against this exact bundle.
3. Verify an isolated deployment root and record final source-use and dataset
   publication decisions before any public release.

## Post-review amendment

The state and object summaries now say “source-linked or atlas-geometry” and
explain that atlas geometry does not prove physical passage, addressing B2's
ambiguous “non-unresolved” wording. The site now includes the existing OSW
56-state equirectangular ground with credited Natural Earth land, while keeping
locators in matching map coordinates; G1's missing-ground concern is addressed
for this review map. A short live status announces object selection without
reading the entire detail panel, addressing H2's implementation concern.
Human visual and screen-reader review and the publication boundary remain open.

## State-passport follow-up — 2026-09-30

The 56 state passports now expose source-reported local current presence,
schematic crossings, editorial locator candidates, and unresolved physical
crossings as separate inspectable lists. Named eddy locator candidates remain
separate from unresolved dated-footprint intersections. Each passport has a
direct `?state=` address; the Chromium check opens one from a fresh page and
checks all evidence-group headings. This improves the transparency requested
by CURRENT, CHART, and BEACON, but it does not establish whole-current passage
or dated eddy containment. The NEEDS-WORK verdict remains unchanged.

## Length-inventory follow-up — 2026-09-30

The screened atlas now shows a searchable row for each of its 95 current
records. Only six published estimates receive the source-set rank; five
geographic floors, one published lower bound, and one proposed system length
are labelled separately. The remaining 82 rows do not invent a whole-current
number. The browser check verifies the class counts and the California source
warning in both the rank and all-current views. This improves CURRENT and
CHART's scope concern without converting the classes into comparable lengths.

## Accessibility follow-up — 2026-09-30

The built bundle now passes automated checks for accessible names on primary
controls, map and length table; skip-link focus; object-heading focus after
selection; short state-selection announcement; and no document-level overflow

routes are in `almanac/release/SCREENED-ATLAS-ACCESSIBILITY-REVIEW.md`.
