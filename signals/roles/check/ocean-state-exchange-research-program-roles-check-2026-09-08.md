## Artifact identification

The proposal advances OSW from source-backed province geometry and sampled
water-column capacity toward adjacency, continuous hypsometry, hydrographic
contents, boundary exchange, dynamic stability, event routes, and eventual
zoning dispositions. It also introduces longitudinal ocean-state accounts as
a way to compare inventories, flows, changes, balances, relationships, shocks,
and revisions through time without asserting impermeable containers.

## Role selection

- **CURRENT** — the plan tests physical identity, transport, heat, controls,
  and causal limits.
- **SOUNDER** — the proposed accounts combine geometry, bathymetry,
  hydrography, velocity, time, depth, uncertainty, and revisions.
- **CHART** — the primary products are adjacency, border-first, dynamic-edge,
  and longitudinal scientific views.
- **BEACON** — “ocean states” and the economic-accounting analogy require a
  clear public meaning and equally visible limits.
- **HARBOR** — maps, timelines, controls, passports, and evidence changes need
  equivalent nonvisual and keyboard paths.
- **KEEL** — topology, source admission, transformations, accounts, and display
  artifacts require deterministic offline verification.
- **LOGBOOK** — the plan changes the central roadmap while remaining proposed,
  uncommitted, and unauthorized as an active goal.

**ORBIT was not selected.** The plan makes no Earth–gas-giant transfer claim.
Any later planetary comparison must receive a separate ORBIT review using
valid pressure, mass, forcing, stratification, rotation, and observation
contracts.
