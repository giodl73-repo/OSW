## PHASE 2 — WEB EVIDENCE

### Claim 1 — A declared full-ocean partition is possible

- Query: `global ocean biogeochemical provinces partition Longhurst`
  - Source: https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/gbc.20089
  - Direct quote: “The partition into 56 biogeochemical provinces proposed by Longhurst [2007] has been widely used.”
  - Relevance: A complete surface-ocean partition already exists for an ecological/biogeochemical purpose.
- Query: `objective global ocean biogeographic provinces`
  - Source: https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2008GL034238
  - Direct quote: “The province concept has provided a framework to spatially aggregate or separate data.”
  - Relevance: Alternative objective classifications can reproduce much of the same broad topology.
- Verdict: CONFIRMED

### Claim 2 — The boundaries are not uniquely fixed

- Query: `Longhurst province boundaries not fixed space time`
  - Source: https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2008GL034238
  - Direct quote: “province boundaries ... are not fixed in space and time and merely represent the mean location.”
  - Relevance: A total partition can be useful without being a permanent material division.
- Query: `dynamic biogeochemical provinces global ocean static partition dynamic`
  - Source: https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/gbc.20089
  - Direct quote: “allowed the static partition to become more dynamic in space and time.”
  - Relevance: Province membership depends on the selected variables and date.
- Verdict: CONFIRMED

### Claim 3 — Coherent flow structures are conditional but physically diagnosable

- Query: `Lagrangian coherent structures ocean finite time objective material surface`
  - Source: https://doi.org/10.1016/j.ocemod.2017.11.008
  - Direct quote: “applicable over a finite time interval” and “describe an actual material surface.”
  - Relevance: Transport structures can be physical while still depending on a declared time window.
- Query: `Lagrangian ocean coherent structures persistent material strain`
  - Source: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-marine-120710-100819
  - Direct quote: “Lagrangian regime boundaries may often be indicated as material curves.”
  - Relevance: The ocean contains organizing transport skeletons, not only named static regions.
- Verdict: CONFIRMED

### Claim 4 — Giant planets contain structurally free gas

- Query: `Jupiter Saturn weather layers jets vortices turbulence review`
  - Source: https://doi.org/10.1146/annurev-fluid-121021-040058
  - Direct quote: “zonally coherent jets and cloud bands extend” while “large-scale vortices ... are relatively shallow.”
  - Relevance: Multiple structures coexist at different depths and scales.
- Query: `Jupiter Saturn coherent structures turbulence gas giant simulation`
  - Source: https://www.nature.com/articles/ngeo2601
  - Direct quote: “deep-seated zonal jets and shallow vortices.”
  - Relevance: Organized jets and vortices sit within a turbulent continuous atmosphere.
- Verdict: UNCONFIRMED. The literature supports organized structures plus turbulent background, but not a physically distinct category of “free gas.”

## PHASE 4 — UNGROUNDED CLAIM

- Claim: some giant-planet gas exists outside every structure as a distinct free-gas class.
- Searches: gas-giant weather-layer reviews and coherent-structure simulations.
- Why unconfirmable: structure membership changes with scale, variable, depth,
  and averaging interval; turbulence is a regime, not absence of physics.
- Alternative: define an explicit planetary coordinate/variable/time ontology,
  then test whether every modeled grid cell receives at least one classification.

## PHASE 5 — AMEND

1. Replace “one natural partition” with “a stack of declared complete covers.”
2. Treat transition/mixed/background as valid diagnosed classes rather than blanks.
3. Build the system in four dimensions—longitude, latitude, depth/pressure, and
   time—because a surface map cannot cover deep water or a deep atmosphere.
