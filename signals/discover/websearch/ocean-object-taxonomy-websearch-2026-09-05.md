# Web grounding — ocean objects, current research, and the OSW opportunity

## Claims to ground

| # | Claim | Source of claim | Why it matters |

## Web evidence

### Claim 1 — established object families

- Query: `ocean fronts eddies jets coherent structures objective detection`
  - Source: [Prants 2025](https://www.frontiersin.org/journals/marine-science/articles/10.3389/fmars.2025.1621820/full)
  - Direct quote: “detect coherent structures, transport barriers, eddies and fronts”
  - Relevance: modern Lagrangian oceanography explicitly treats these as detectable organizing structures.
- Query: `global ocean water mass transformation framework heat transport`
  - Source: [Chen et al. 2025](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2025JC022445)
  - Direct quote: “volume flux from one water mass with a specific state ... to another”
  - Relevance: water masses and transformation are a separate property-space object tradition.
- Verdict: **CONFIRMED**.

### Claim 2 — object identity is dynamic and scale-dependent

- Query: `Southern Ocean no uniquely accepted definition eddies jets fronts`
  - Source: [Bennetts et al. 2024](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1029/2022RG000781)
  - Direct quote: “There is no uniquely accepted definition of eddies, jets or fronts.”
  - Relevance: directly supports plural definitions and coupled features.
- Query: `ocean eddy no universally accepted best method detection`
  - Source: [Kurian et al. 2011](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2010JC006895)
  - Direct quote: “There is no universally accepted best method for detecting an eddy.”
  - Relevance: objective algorithms still embody definitions and thresholds.
- Query: `dynamic biogeochemical provinces global ocean 56`
  - Source: [Reygondeau et al. 2013](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1002/gbc.20089)
  - Direct quote: “static boundaries ... do not respond to the temporal dynamics of the ocean”
  - Relevance: even exhaustive province maps must distinguish reference geography from moving state.
- Verdict: **CONFIRMED**.

### Claim 3 — correspondence with current Nordic research

- Query: `2026 Nordic Seas Atlantic Water branches heat transport Fram Barents`
  - Source: [Koenigk et al. 2026](https://os.copernicus.org/articles/22/2375/2026/)
  - Direct quote: “the flow splits up in two branches”
  - Relevance: independently describes branching, atmospheric cooling, recirculation, and two Arctic routes.
- Query: `2026 Norwegian Atlantic Current cooling observations`
  - Source: [Baumann et al. 2026](https://os.copernicus.org/articles/22/17/2026/)
  - Direct quote: “a principal conduit for poleward heat and salt transport”
  - Relevance: confirms the conduit/transformation interpretation from 30 years of observations.
- Query: `2026 Arctic overturning transformations pathways timescales Atlantic Water`
  - Source: [Arctic overturning circulation 2026](https://os.copernicus.org/articles/22/565/2026/)
  - Direct quote: “surface forcing in the Barents Sea and north of Svalbard dominates dense overturning”
  - Relevance: supports the relay-plus-transformation framing and distinct branch outcomes.
- Verdict: **CONFIRMED**.

### Claim 4 — gas-giant analogy

- Query: `meridional heat transport planetary atmospheres oceans Jupiter Southern Ocean jets`
  - Source: [Gallet and Ferrari 2021](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2020AV000362)
  - Direct quote: “banded jets observed in gaseous planets ... and ... the Southern Ocean”
  - Relevance: the comparison is explicit in quantitative geophysical-fluid-dynamics research.
- Query: `Jupiter Saturn weather layers geostrophic turbulence vortices zonal jets review`
  - Source: [Showman et al. 2024](https://www.annualreviews.org/content/journals/10.1146/annurev-fluid-121021-040058)
  - Direct quote: “zonally coherent jets and cloud bands extend”
  - Relevance: gas-giant objects include deep jets, bands, vortices, and distinct vertical extents.
- Query: `ocean giant planet jet Rhines scale uncertainty`
  - Source: [Wang et al. 2012](https://agupubs.onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/full/10.1029/2012GL051861)
  - Direct quote: “It is still unclear to what extent”
  - Relevance: shared dynamics do not make every terrestrial jet Jovian or establish a common taxonomy.
- Verdict: **CONFIRMED**, with strict limits.

### Claim 5 — a unified OSW-style atlas is absent

- Query: `global daily ocean front dataset atlas`
  - Source: [Gao et al. 2025](https://essd.copernicus.org/articles/17/2831/2025/index.html)
  - Direct quote: “first publicly available global ocean front dataset covering the past 42 years”
  - Relevance: a major global atlas exists for one object family: fronts.
- Query: `NOAA MBON dynamic pelagic seascape classification`
  - Source: [NOAA CoastWatch MBON Seascapes](https://coastwatch.star.nesdis.noaa.gov/cwn/products/seascape-pelagic-habitat-classification.html)
  - Direct quote: “describe dynamic, changing ocean habitats”
  - Relevance: a routinely generated global classification exists for another family: habitats.
- Query: `global coherent ocean regions transfer operator`
  - Source: [Froyland et al. 2007](https://doi.org/10.1103/PHYSREVLETT.98.224503)
  - Direct quote: “uncover coherent regions in the surface ocean”
  - Relevance: transport-defined regions also have a mature mathematical lineage.
- Verdict: **UNCONFIRMED**. The search found strong single-family atlases but cannot prove no integrative project exists.

- **Searches attempted:** global ocean object atlas; ocean coherent-region atlas; global front dataset; dynamic ocean provinces; transport network atlas.
